## Overnight Developments

### [Nvidia stock falls 8% as investors await Jensen Huang's fate](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-nvda-jensen-huang-second-affair.html) — CNBC

The Nvidia CEO scandal widened overnight. On Wednesday The New York Times reported a second extramarital relationship, this one with Kimberly Powell, an Nvidia vice president who reports to Huang. CNBC confirmed that Nvidia's board met Wednesday to discuss whether to remove him. On Sunday Huang admitted an affair with Alice Huang, a Megaspeed executive whom U.S. intelligence officials reportedly believe is a Chinese spy. Bernstein cut its Nvidia price target in half, from $400 to $200, and Stacy Rasgon said the direct-report relationship makes it "much tougher for the board to leave him in position." Several analysts told CNBC the stock could fall another 20% if Huang is removed. The stock is already down more than 20% over the past month.
Affected sectors: Semiconductors, AI infrastructure, Mega-cap tech

### [Jensen Huang affair in DC: Resignation calls and moves to tighten Nvidia China exports](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/11/nvidia-jensen-huang-congress-resign-ai-chips.html) — CNBC

Pressure is also building in Washington. Sen. Kevin Cramer (R-N.D.) and Reps. Suhas Subramanyam (D-Va.) and Don Bacon (R-Neb.) have called for Huang to resign. House committee chairs Mast and Moolenaar said they would consider a subpoena if he does not agree to testify on Nov. 18. The headline risk now covers China export policy as well as governance.
Affected sectors: Semiconductors, AI infrastructure

## Breaking News & Macro Events

### [Fed holds rates steady despite concerns over inflation](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/28/fed-interest-rate-decision-october-2026.html) — CNBC
The FOMC kept the funds rate at **3.75%–4%**, where it has been since September's quarter-point hike. Two of 12 voters dissented in favor of another hike: Governor **Lisa Cook** (her first dissent since joining in 2022) and Cleveland's **Beth Hammack**. The statement upgraded growth to "expanding at a strong pace" from "solid" and kept "inflation remains elevated." Chair Kevin Warsh: "There is no soft inflation target," per the [CNBC Investing Club](https://www.cnbc.com/investingclub/2026/10/28/fed-chairman-talks-tough-on-inflation-plus-whats-behind-microns-big-rally.html). He also said one 2027 meeting will be set aside for strategy, with no rate vote. CME FedWatch still puts the odds of a December hike at **85.7%**. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent [said](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/28/scott-bessent-interview.html) another hike would "not necessarily" be a mistake.

### [10-year Treasury yield creeps higher even as Fed keeps rates the same](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/28/us-treasurys-bonds-yields-federal-reserve.html) — CNBC
The curve steepened through the hold. The 10-year closed at **5.05%** (^TNX, +5 bp) and the 30-year rose more than 4 bp to 5.354%, while the 2-year slipped more than 3 bp to 4.704%. The long end sold off even as front-end yields eased, which fits a stronger growth outlook with no hike. Thursday's PCE, Q3 GDP and personal income are the next test of whether December is locked in.

### [Oil rises as U.S. reportedly tells Gulf countries to prepare for Iran strikes after the midterms](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/28/oil-price-today-wti-brent-hormuz.html) — CNBC
WTI settled up **2.9% at $102.78** and Brent up **1.3% at $115.32**. The Wall Street Journal reported that Washington told several Gulf states to be ready for "a short but intense military campaign" after the Nov. 3 elections. The Houthis also said they had hit Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery with a ballistic missile. This reverses Tuesday's slide on Iranian peace-condition headlines, and an oil spike is the one thing Jeff Gundlach says could still stop a December hike (see Notable Voices).

### [Alphabet's free cash flow sinks further; capex misses](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/28/alphabet-googl-earnings-q3-2026.html) — CNBC
Revenue grew **25%**, the fastest since 2021, and Cloud revenue hit **$30.7 billion** against $28.7 billion expected. Gemini reached 1 billion users. But EPS was **$2.12** against a $3.02 LSEG estimate (CNBC says comparability isn't clear) after an **$8.66 billion** loss on equity holdings. Search revenue of $63.35 billion missed $64.96 billion, and operating income of $40.58 billion missed $43.19 billion. Free cash flow fell to **-$9.07 billion** against -$2.70 billion expected. Capex was $51.36 billion against $55.09 billion expected. Microsoft, by contrast, reported Azure revenue of **$33.17 billion** (+48%) and kept free cash flow positive at $18.63 billion. Investors are now sorting the hyperscalers by cash generation, not only AI growth.

### [Nvidia's $12.9 billion Hugging Face deal draws DOJ scrutiny](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/28/doj-questions-nvidias-12point9-billion-hugging-face-deal.html) — CNBC
The Justice Department has asked Nvidia how the September acquisition could affect competitors; Reuters reported it first and CNBC confirmed it. It is Nvidia's second-biggest purchase, after the **$20 billion** Groq asset deal, which has also drawn DOJ scrutiny. Nvidia fell 1.2%.

## Market Outlook

The Fed held at 3.75%–4% with two hawkish dissents, and markets took it calmly: the S&P 500 rose 0.28% for a third straight gain and the VIX fell to 16.91. The hold did not ease rates: the 10-year closed above 5% and FedWatch still puts a December hike at roughly 85%. Oil's 2.9% jump on reports of renewed Iran strikes after the election is the input most likely to settle that December decision. After the bell, Alphabet and Microsoft showed what investors now reward. Microsoft's Azure grew 48% and it kept free cash flow positive, while Alphabet's free cash flow was negative, which suggests caution toward hyperscalers funding AI capex from the balance sheet. Thursday brings PCE and Q3 GDP in the morning and Amazon, Intel and Strategy after the close, and Amazon's free-cash-flow guidance deserves the closest attention.

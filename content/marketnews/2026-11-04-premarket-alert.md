## Overnight Developments

### [Democrats flip both chambers of Congress in midterm blue wave](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/04/2026-midterm-election-results-live-updates.html) — CNBC
Democrats flipped both the House and the Senate. As of early Wednesday, the CNBC Election Desk projected them to win at least 250 House seats and at least 54 Senate seats; Alaska and Mississippi are the only Senate races still uncalled. The majorities give Democrats subpoena power and a shot at a binding [War Powers Resolution on Iran](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/04/us-midterm-elections-iran-war-speaker.html). Iran's parliament speaker reacted on X that Americans "said NO TO WAR."

Futures [fell as results came in](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/03/treasury-yields-fall-as-democrats-win-big-in-us-midterm-elections.html), with AI shares leading the overnight decline on fears that Democrats would slow the data-center buildout. The 10-year yield slipped 3 bp to about 4.92% as money moved into bonds. Futures then reversed and are now solidly higher.
Affected sectors: Broad market, AI/Data centers, Defense, Energy, Rates

### [SpaceX and Tesla slide as Musk's $474M election bet backfires](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/04/ai-stocks-texas-governor-data-centers.html) — CNBC
Democrats won both the Texas Senate seat (Talarico over Paxton) and the governor's mansion (Hinojosa over Abbott). That is a direct hit to Musk, who put $224 million into Texas alone. Dan Ives called Texas the "biggest worry" of the night for the AI trade, since that is where around 25%–30% of planned data-center capacity is set to come from. Tesla is down about 6% pre-market from Tuesday's close. Big Tech is holding up: Amazon, Alphabet and Meta are all higher.
Affected sectors: AI infrastructure, EV/Autos, Space

### [Euro zone inflation hits 4%, a three-year high](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/04/euro-zone-cpi-inflation-october-ecb-rate-hike.html) — CNBC
October flash CPI rose to 4.0% from 3.8%, in line with the Reuters poll. Energy inflation climbed to 20.8% on the back of the Iran war. Markets now price a more than 72% chance that the ECB hikes in December. The euro is up 0.6% against the dollar.
Affected sectors: Rates, FX, Energy

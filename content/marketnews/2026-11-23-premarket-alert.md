## Overnight Developments

### [Anthropic, Nvidia shares rise in premarket trading after major tech selloff](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/anthropic-nvidia-shares.html) — CNBC
After the Nasdaq's worst week since 2020 (down 10% last week, per CNBC), stock futures are pointing to a strong rebound. Nvidia was up 8.6% in early trading and Anthropic up 4.47% ahead of its third trading day; the stock closed 8.5% lower on Friday after its IPO debut on Thursday. CNBC also reported pre-market gains for Alphabet (+4.98%), Meta (+4.36%), Amazon (+3.96%) and AMD (+5.77%), with neoclouds such as Nebius and CoreWeave up roughly 10%. This is a bounce after heavy selling, not a change in the underlying story: Nvidia is still more than 40% below where it was a month ago.
Affected sectors: Tech, Semiconductors, AI infrastructure

### [U.S. probes Megaspeed, Alice Huang as new figure emerges in Nvidia chip case](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/nvidia-megaspeed-chipmaker-us.html) — CNBC
On Sunday, DNI Jay Clayton confirmed that an investigation into Megaspeed and Alice Huang is ongoing. Treasury Secretary Bessent said the administration is seeking answers from Nvidia about who oversaw compliance on chip sales to China. This headline risk is still live under today's rebound, for NVDA and for SMCI, whose servers are named in the Singapore charges.
Affected sectors: Semiconductors, AI servers

### [CrowdStrike to buy Zscaler for $43.9 billion](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/crowdstrike-to-buy-zscaler.html) — CNBC
The all-stock deal gives Zscaler holders 1.135 CRWD shares per share, implying $260.41, a 34% premium. ZS is up about 16% pre-market and CRWD down about 9%, according to our 07:00 ET quotes. CrowdStrike also guided Q4 revenue above consensus.
Affected sectors: Cybersecurity, Software

### [Treasury yields jump as stock futures rebound](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/treasury-yields-bonds-fed.html) — CNBC
The 2-year yield is up 7 bp to 4.689% and the 10-year is up 4 bp to 4.959%. According to CME FedWatch, cited by CNBC, markets now price about a **96%** chance of a December rate *hike*, up from about 92% on Friday. NY Fed President John Williams speaks later today. Revised Q3 GDP and October core PCE are due Wednesday.
Affected sectors: Rate-sensitives, Financials, Housing

### [Houthis claim attack on Saudi Rabigh refinery](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/trump-iran-war-hormuz-saudi-.html) — CNBC
On Sunday, Yemen's Houthis said they hit Saudi Arabia's Rabigh refinery. The extent of any damage is unclear, and the Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted a drone. Gulf states reported multiple attacks by Iran-linked forces over the weekend.
Affected sectors: Energy, Airlines, Defense

## Overnight Developments

### [Iran escalates in Hormuz as a third U.S. carrier arrives](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/17/iran-war-trump-hormuz-oil-.html) — CNBC
Iran's Revolutionary Guards Navy told shippers the U.S.-backed southern route through the Strait of Hormuz is fully closed, calling it a "minefield of fire." On Tuesday the U.K. Maritime Trade Operations agency said an unidentified projectile struck a tanker in the strait and set it on fire. The USS Theodore Roosevelt joined two other U.S. carriers in the Gulf on Sunday. Trump, who [landed in Manila](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/17/trump-iran-asia-trip-china.html) for his Asia trip, said his administration is "very close to getting it done" on Iran. Oil barely reacted: December WTI is up about **0.6%** to **$86.27**.
Affected sectors: Energy, shipping, defense

### [Ukraine damages the Kerch Bridge to Crimea](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/17/kerch-bridge-ukraine-russia-putin-kyiv.html) — CNBC
Crimea's Russia-installed governor confirmed that the bridge was attacked again overnight. Footage circulating online appears to show one section collapsed into the water, though CNBC could not verify it. Zelenskyy called it an "unprecedented operation."
Affected sectors: Energy, European defense

### [Nvidia warns chip buyers of China-access legal risk ahead of tonight's earnings](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/17/nvidia-china-cloud-ai-chip-access.html) — CNBC
Sources told CNBC that Nvidia has warned customers the proposed Remote Access Security Act could expose them to U.S. "legal action." It also said it won't sell to customers who rent more than 50% of their GPUs to Chinese companies. Nvidia reports Q3 after the close, and [options are pricing a **7%** move](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/17/nvidia-has-lost-2-trillion-in-value-heres-what-options-traders-expect-from-earnings.html), the largest implied move into a report since February 2025.
Affected sectors: Semiconductors, AI hardware

## Breaking News & Macro Events

### [Tech leads a Black Friday rally to cap a volatile week](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) — CNBC (Sean Conlon)
Meta rose more than 3%, Amazon about 4% and Broadcom almost 4%. Anthropic, which went public last week, added nearly 11%. Among the [day's movers](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/stocks-moving-the-most-today-live-updates.html), Sandisk was the S&P 500's best performer, Newmont gained 4.1% as gold, silver, platinum and palladium all jumped, and XP surged nearly 18.5% after Lula named Bradesco Chairman Luiz Carlos Trabuco as Brazil's next finance minister. Vertex slid more than 7% after the FDA extended its review by up to three months. CNBC's Fred Imbert [cautioned](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/this-weeks-bounce-following-a-rapid-correction-may-not-hold-history-shows.html) that history argues against the bounce lasting. Capital Economics' Jonas Goltermann counted 11 prior 10% drops from an all-time high within two months: five became bear markets and four others were near misses. "The odds in favor of [Nov. 20] marking the turnround point are not particularly good," he wrote.

### [Chinese chip stocks post their biggest one-day gain on record](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/china-chip-stocks-beijing-market-support.html) — CNBC (Evelyn Cheng)
The CSI Semiconductor index jumped **17.6%**, its largest daily gain ever per Wind, and the STAR index surged 14%, led by CXMT, Cambricon and SMIC. The CSI 300 rose 4%, its biggest jump since October 2024, a day after it closed at its lowest since the spring 2025 tariff escalation. The trigger was a State Council meeting under Premier Li Qiang, whose readout pledged to strengthen the role of the state-backed "National Team" funds in stabilizing markets. The Hang Seng rose 2%.

### [Nvidia chip buyers told to rule out Chinese renters](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/neoclouds-nvidia-china-ai.html) — CNBC (Kai Nicol-Schwarz)
Sources told CNBC that chip sellers are telling neoclouds they cannot buy Nvidia GPUs if any of that capacity will be rented to Chinese companies. This follows the BIS rule on remote access that took effect Nov. 19. Before the rule, Nvidia's own threshold had been customers renting more than 50% of their GPUs to Chinese firms. The [Tech Download](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/tech-download-jensen-huang-ai-market.html) notes that Nvidia lost more than $1 trillion in value and regained just under $1 trillion during the Huang saga. Freedom Capital Markets' Egor Tolmachev expects Nvidia to "trade sideways and stay volatile below its October peak at least until the Dec 1–3 Congressional appearances."

### [Trump calls the GDP revision a "RECORD SETTING ERROR"](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/trump-gdp-revision-investigation.html) — CNBC (Sean Conlon)
On Thanksgiving, Trump said the upward revision of Q3 GDP, from 3.4% to **4.3%**, should be "immediately investigated." He suggested the advance estimate, released just before the midterms, had influenced the outcome. CNBC called the claim false. The 0.9-point revision was the largest since Q2 2020 but tied for 16th among advance-to-second revisions since 1990, about double the 0.43-point average.

### [Tariff threats push partners toward deals](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/us-tariffs-hike-trade-agreement.html) — CNBC (Anniek Bao)
The proposed Section 301 "overcapacity" tariffs start at 15% for the EU, South Korea, Switzerland and Taiwan and run as high as **35% for Vietnam**. China faces a capped 20% on top of the existing 25%, which could rise to 40% if the trade truce isn't extended beyond January. A senior U.S. official said the duties could take effect as early as January. China's Commerce Ministry told Washington to "rectify its wrongdoing."

### [Iran tensions flare after deaths in Kuwait](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-italian-killed-trump.html) — CNBC (Lim Hui Jie)
Kuwait's army said two Italians were killed by falling missile debris from Iranian attacks; Tehran denied involvement. An Israeli military spokesman said Israel was prepared to strike Iran "at any moment," even without U.S. forces. Separately, CNBC's [live blog](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) reports that Saudi Arabia said its Jazan refinery was attacked, killing at least one worker. That came after Wednesday's Houthi claim of a strike on Ras Tanura.

## Notable Voices

### [Jim Cramer: Huang's back, but AI jitters remain; buying Alphabet on weakness](https://www.cnbc.com/investingclub/2026/11/27/jensen-huangs-return-did-little-to-ease-ai-jitters-we-bought-on-weakness.html) — Jim Cramer, CNBC Investing Club (2026-11-27)
"Jensen is the linchpin that holds the AI solar system together, and Nvidia is the biggest beneficiary," Cramer said on Monday's Mad Money. He argued that Nvidia deserves roughly 12 times earnings, a $199 price target. His charitable trust bought 50 shares of Alphabet on Tuesday's weakness and upgraded it to a buy-equivalent rating, citing a valuation just below 19 times 2027 earnings. It also bought Goldman Sachs on Wednesday. The club's S&P Oscillator moved deeper into oversold territory despite Monday's rally, to −8.75%. The club notes that about half the time the Oscillator hits −8% it goes on to −10%, "historically an excellent buying opportunity."

### [Henry Allen (Deutsche Bank): a correction lacking bear-market triggers](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/the-sp-500-is-in-a-correction-bear-markets-and-recessions-dont-always-follow.html) — via CNBC (2026-11-27)
Allen notes that every S&P 500 bear market since 1962 has coincided with a recession, worsening data that raised recession fears, or a cycle of Fed hikes. CNBC added that "None of those are present today, at least so far." "Much as last week's selloff was severe, it's worth remembering that it has several differences with many of the other big risk-off moves in recent times," Allen wrote. One caveat: futures price a 98% chance of a December hike, so the Fed-hike leg of his test may not stay clear for long.

### [Ben Fulton (WEBs Investments): don't be short AI](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) — CNBC live blog (2026-11-27)
Fulton called the AI fears "a bit overblown," with the exception of those about debt financing, and said the "biggest risk" is whether AI companies can "keep funding the growth they need." "The risk seems to be on the upside right now," he said. "You don't want to be short this market, at least the AI names."

## Market Outlook

Black Friday's half-session finished a remarkable week. The S&P 500 rose 1.6% to close near its high, and the week's 5.6% gain was its best since April 2025, while the VIX fell a third to 23.81. Strength was broad: every watchlist name closed higher, and the most beaten-down groups led, with nuclear names up about 7% and Rocket Lab up 8.2%. Nvidia, the stock that started the rally, added just 0.8%. That points to a short-covering bounce from oversold levels more than a renewed conviction in AI, which is why Capital Economics' warning, that most rapid 10% drawdowns either became bear markets or came close, warrants attention. The macro backdrop has not eased: the 10-year rose 9 bp to 4.94%, the dollar sits near a 52-week high, and FedWatch puts the odds of a Dec. 9 hike at 98%. The next test comes in a dense window: Huang's congressional appearances on Dec. 1 and 3, then the November jobs report on Dec. 4, which will show whether the rebound can hold up against a Fed that is still tightening.

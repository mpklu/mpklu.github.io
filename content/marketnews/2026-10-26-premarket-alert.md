## Overnight Developments

### [Meta files an automatic shelf registration for stock, preferred, warrants and units](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000119312526431009/0001193125-26-431009-index.htm) — SEC EDGAR
Meta filed an S-3ASR at **6:41:26 a.m. ET**, and the stock broke in the same minute: from $640.61 at 6:39 to $632.92 at 6:41, then a second leg to a **$616.92 low (-4.0%)** at 6:59 before bouncing to about $629.60 (-2.0%) by 7:04. The prospectus, dated today, says Meta "may, from time to time, offer and sell, in one or more offerings, shares of common stock, preferred stock, depositary shares, warrants, purchase contracts, or units." It is an unsized well-known-seasoned-issuer shelf with no prospectus supplement yet, so there is no offering size or timing on file. It follows last week's board action creating a new non-voting share class, which the filings said is meant to [boost the company's ability to raise capital for AI infrastructure](https://abcnews.com/Technology/wireStory/zuckerberg-makes-deal-meta-pay-billionaire-tax-passes-137488685) and to let Mark Zuckerberg sell shares to cover a potential California billionaire tax. Meta had already [lost 9% last week](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/23/meta-shares-rough-week-mike-khouw-is-buying.html) on AI buildout spending worries despite an earnings beat.
Affected sectors: Tech, AI Infrastructure

### [Lula wins a fourth term; Bolsonaro refuses to concede and Brazilian ADRs crater](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/26/brazil-lula-win-presidential-election-runoff-vote.html) — CNBC
Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva took **51.8% to 48.2%** in Sunday's runoff after trailing in the first round, the first such comeback since Brazil returned to direct elections in 1989. Flávio Bolsonaro says he will seek to annul the result. Markets had positioned for a Bolsonaro win and fiscal austerity: the iShares MSCI Brazil ETF (EWZ) is **-25%** and Petrobras **-23%** pre-market, with CNBC's live blog showing Itaú Unibanco -26%, Ambev -19%, Vale -3.2% and Embraer -4%. U.S. index futures are not reacting; this is an emerging-markets and Latin America story so far.
Affected sectors: Emerging Markets, Energy, Materials, Banks

### [Neocloud stocks fall as Elon Musk says more than 200 of them face 'guppy-level death'](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/26/neocloud-stocks-today-coreweave-nebius-iren-elon-musk.html) — CNBC
Musk, replying to a Chamath Palihapitiya post predicting "partially stranded compute" in 2027, said more than 200 companies in the sector could collapse. CoreWeave is **-5.0%**, Nebius **-3.9%** and Iren **-3.9%** pre-market on top of October losses of more than 30% each, as credit investors have pushed their bond yields to distressed levels. Nvidia, an investor in several neoclouds, is flat. Separately, Musk said SpaceX closed **$40 billion** in callable debt financing to buy GPUs, structured through a special purpose vehicle to keep it off the balance sheet, per Bloomberg.
Affected sectors: AI Infrastructure, Semiconductors, Credit

### [Oil slides over 2% as traders weigh Saudi attack risks, demand destruction](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/26/oil-prices-today-wti-brent.html) — CNBC
WTI is **-2.19% to $101** and Brent **-1.89% to $114.51** after Friday's rally took Brent to a six-month-high settle of $116.72. The Houthis said they [targeted Riyadh's main airport and oil facilities](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/25/saudi-arabia-houthi-missile-drone-interceptions.html) on Saturday and Saudi Arabia reported intercepting more missiles and drones on Sunday, but the airport resumed operations and the market is now weighing demand erosion above $120 Brent. The post-midterm U.S.-Israel strike risk on Iran that drove last week's move has not changed.
Affected sectors: Energy, Airlines, Transports

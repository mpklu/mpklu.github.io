## Breaking News & Macro Events

### [Nvidia stock closes down over 4% after CEO Jensen Huang announces resignation](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-nvda-jensen-huang-second-affair.html) — CNBC
Jensen Huang is stepping down immediately as CEO and as a board member. CFO **Colette Kress** becomes interim CEO, and former Synopsys CEO **Harvey C. Jones** becomes Nvidia's first-ever board chair. The departure follows Sunday's admission of an affair with Megaspeed executive Alice Huang, whom U.S. intelligence officials reportedly believe is a Chinese spy. On Wednesday The New York Times reported a second relationship, with a direct report. NVDA fell as much as 14% and hit a 52-week low before closing **-4.34%**, back above a $4 trillion market cap. Bernstein halved its price target from $400 to $200. In a separate exclusive, CNBC reported that [Alice Huang may have held stakes in businesses that benefited Huawei](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/alice-huang-jensen-huang-nvidia-huawei.html), Nvidia's biggest competitor in China.

### [Jensen Huang's affair with alleged spy revives push to tighten China chip curbs ahead of Trump-Xi summit](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-trump-jensen-chip-export-controls-china-xi-.html) — CNBC
Huang was "the industry's most effective lobbyist against tighter rules," said Eurasia Group's David Meale. With him gone, Meale said it is now "politically harder for negotiators to strip chip provisions out of this year's NDAA." The bills in play are the AI Overwatch Act, the Chip Security Act and the MATCH Act. Trump meets Xi next week, so the China-revenue risk for NVDA, TSM and the AI-server names now runs through Congress as well as the boardroom.

### [Oil prices rise after Trump says no Iran deal needed: 'We've got their Strait'](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/oil-prices-jump-after-trump-says-no-iran-deal-needed-weve-got-their-strait.html) — CNBC
"We don't need a deal with Iran. Those days have long since passed," Trump posted on Truth Social. That reversed part of the peace premium priced in since the midterms. WTI [settled at **$88.74 (+2.55%)**, and Brent at **$95.82 (+3.3%)**](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/11/stock-market-today-live-updates.html). Even after today, CNBC notes that WTI is down 16% in November and Brent 20%. Higher energy prices land badly two days after a hot CPI print.

### [In a policy switch, the Fed says its October 2027 meeting will not see a vote on interest rates](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/in-policy-switch-fed-says-its-october-2027-meeting-will-not-see-a-vote-on-interest-rates.html) — CNBC
Chair Kevin Warsh's Fed will hold seven voting meetings in 2027 and set aside the Oct. 26–27 session for strategy discussion. The year's final decision moves to November. On the nearer-term path, Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack [said on the session live blog](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/11/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) that she still supports hikes but prefers "smaller increments, more frequent." She does not think the Fed is behind the curve. That is a softer line than Governor Waller's Tuesday case for 50 bp. Chicago's Goolsbee had called Tuesday's CPI ["borderline disturbing"](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/11/goolsbee-october-inflation-fed-rates-disturbing-distressing.html).

### [Data: claims tick up, existing home sales hit a 16-year low](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/11/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) — CNBC
Initial jobless claims rose 9,000 to **200,000** for the week ending Nov. 7, above the 195,000 consensus. Continuing claims fell 20,000 to just under 1.7 million. October existing home sales fell **5.5%** to a 3.75 million annualized pace, the lowest since August 2010. NAR's Lawrence Yun blamed mortgage rates that have climbed to "their highest levels since 2000, aside from a single month in 2023." Treasuries reopened after Veterans Day and sold off, with the 10-year closing near **5.10%**.

## Market Outlook

Jensen Huang's resignation was the main event. NVDA fell as much as 14% before closing down 4.3%, and the S&P 500 lost only 0.35% as money rotated into AMD, Broadcom and Alphabet. The rest of the tape was weaker than the headline averages suggest. The Russell 2000 fell 1.66%, every speculative name on the watchlist (nuclear, crypto, SMCI) was down 3% or more, and the 10-year closed near 5.10% after the bond market reopened. Oil rebounded more than 2.5% on Trump's Iran comments. Taken together, that suggests caution: rate pressure is building again while the market's AI leader has no permanent CEO. Friday's PPI is the next test of whether Hammack's "gradual" camp or Waller's 50 bp camp prevails in December. After that comes next week's cluster: Nvidia's scheduled Nov. 17 earnings under an interim CEO, Anthropic's IPO pricing and the Trump-Xi summit.

## Breaking News & Macro Events

### [AI infrastructure stocks slump on calls for an AI slowdown](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/14/ai-stocks-slowdown-amodei-altman.html) — CNBC
Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published a weekend essay, "We Must Pace the Frontier," arguing the industry "must slow the pace at which we improve the capabilities of AI models." Sam Altman and Elon Musk backed him; President Trump pushed back against regulation. The market read it as a threat to the AI capex cycle, and the damage was concentrated in the hardware supply chain: the **SMH semiconductor ETF fell -4.75%**, **SOXX -5.63%**, Micron -5.25%, Intel and Applied Materials more than -4% each, and Nvidia about -3%. Notably, the large-cap AI *consumers* went the other way — GOOG +3.06% and META +2.71% — so this was a supply-chain de-rating, not a market-wide risk-off day.

### [Oil spikes on Saudi pipeline shutdown, then fades](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-today-sept-14-133832281.html) — TheStreet via Yahoo Finance
Fresh attacks on shipping in the Strait of Hormuz and a drone strike prompted Saudi Arabia to temporarily shut its East-West pipeline, the main bypass around Hormuz. Brent surged more than 3% toward $106 in early trading after gaining nearly 9% last week, then gave back most of the intraday move. Settle-to-settle, **WTI finished +1.79% at $101.84** and **Brent +1.54% at $106.22**. Oil is at four-month highs two days before a Fed decision where the market is already leaning toward a hike.

### [10-year yield touches a multiyear high above 5% ahead of the Fed](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/14/10-year-us-treasury-is-closing-in-on-5percent.html) — CNBC
The 10-year Treasury yield reached **5.01% intraday**, its highest since October 2023, before easing to close at **4.96%**. Per [Fundstrat](https://fundstratdirect.com/market-intelligence/fsi-snapshot/2026/09/13/stocks-tumble-as-the-10-year-treasury-yield-edges-close-to-5/), CME FedWatch odds of a **25bp hike on Wednesday jumped to roughly 85%** from 59% a week earlier after Friday's inflation report, and Treasury Secretary Bessent announced $6 billion in longer-dated bond buybacks to lean against the move. The current target range is 3.50–3.75%.

## Market Outlook

Monday was a narrow selloff dressed up as a broad one: the S&P 500 slipped **-0.48%** while the VIX jumped **+7.95%** to 17.10, and nearly all the damage sat in semiconductors and AI hardware (SMH -4.75%) after Dario Amodei's call to slow frontier AI development drew public backing from Altman and Musk. The tell that this is a supply-chain de-rating rather than a growth scare is that GOOG (+3.06%) and META (+2.71%) rallied on the same news, while crude settling higher on the Saudi pipeline shutdown and a 10-year yield that kissed 5.01% intraday kept the macro backdrop tight. With CME FedWatch pricing roughly an 85% chance of a 25bp hike, Wednesday's FOMC decision is the whole week: a hike into $100+ oil and 5% yields would test the AI-consumer names that held up today, while a hold would vindicate Tom Lee's contrarian call and likely spark a sharp relief bid in the very names that sold off. Retail sales land the same morning, so watch whether consumer data gives the Fed cover in either direction.

## Overnight Developments

A broad, synchronized risk-on move across every major region. Three drivers, in order of weight:

### [Oil retreats below $100 on Hormuz supply recovery](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-monday-september-21-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-080214605.html) — Yahoo Finance
Front-month WTI is down **~3.2% to $92.98**, extending a multi-day slide as shipping through the Strait of Hormuz picks up and diplomatic prospects improve. Falling crude is the cleanest inflation-relief signal available right now, and it is doing the heavy lifting for equities.
Affected sectors: Energy (negative), Tech, Consumer, Financials (positive)

### [US–China trade optimism ahead of Thursday's Trump–Xi summit](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-monday-september-21-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-080214605.html) — Yahoo Finance
Treasury Secretary Bessent described preliminary talks with his Chinese counterpart as "very successful" and floated a **new US–China AI dialogue** centered on national-security concerns. The presidents meet **Thursday, Sept 24**, with Jensen Huang and Sam Altman among the tech executives attending a dinner with the Chinese president. This is the most direct tailwind for semis and AI infrastructure.
Affected sectors: Semiconductors, Tech, Manufacturing

### Bitcoin clears $84,000
BTC trades at **$84,511, up ~5%**, after breaking $80,000 on Friday. That single fact explains the two largest watchlist movers this morning.
Affected sectors: Crypto, Fintech

**The counterweight:** the Fed **hiked 25bp on Sept 16**, moving the target range to **3.75%–4.00%** (confirmed via FRED). Traders are pricing the possibility of further hikes and bond yields are rising. Today's rally is happening *despite* a hawkish Fed, not because of one — which makes it more fragile than the breadth suggests.

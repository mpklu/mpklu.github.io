## Overnight Developments

### [Record tanker attacks in the Strait of Hormuz push oil past $100](https://krdo.com/news/2026/10/08/oil-prices-jump-amid-record-tanker-attacks-in-hormuz/) — KRDO (wire), published 2026-10-08 04:34 ET

Kpler counted **10 tankers struck** in the Strait of Hormuz between September 28 and October 4 — a record week, above the previous high of six. In the latest attack, on Wednesday, a tanker reported being hit by "multiple projectiles," causing casualties, according to the UK Maritime Trade Operations agency. Traffic is thinning in response: only **seven tankers transited the strait on Tuesday**, less than half the seven-day average and the fewest since July 23.

Crude has repriced hard. Brent is up **+5.1% to $105.35** and WTI **+5.1% to $92.78** as of 07:00 ET, extending the move reported overnight. The December WTI contract is up 4.8% alongside the November contract, and the curve is in backwardation — the signature of a genuine supply disruption rather than a futures roll artifact.

Affected sectors: Energy (positive), Airlines, Transportation, Semiconductors, Consumer Discretionary.

## What to Watch at Open

The question at the open is whether this is an energy-supply shock the market can compartmentalize or the start of a broader de-risking — watch whether energy strength holds while semis stabilize, or whether selling broadens past the oil-sensitive names. With crude up 5% and the 10-year near 5.28%, the inflation-plus-yields combination is the squeeze point, and any further Hormuz headline during the session is the obvious catalyst for leg two.

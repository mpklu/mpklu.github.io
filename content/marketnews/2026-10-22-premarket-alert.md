## Overnight Developments

### [U.S. pulls diplomats from Saudi Arabia as it reportedly readies Iran strikes after midterms](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/22/us-iran-trump-hormuz-.html) — CNBC
The State Department ordered non-emergency government employees and their families out of Saudi Arabia as Houthi attacks on the kingdom escalate. The New York Times, citing unnamed U.S. officials, reports the U.S. and Israeli militaries have drawn up specific plans for renewed strikes on Iran after the Nov. 3 midterms. This lands a day after Iran's Revolutionary Guards said mines stopped three tankers in the Strait of Hormuz, so the war-risk premium that faded yesterday afternoon is back on.
Affected sectors: Energy, Airlines, Transports, Defense

### [Brent crude tops $111 on Middle East worries as U.S., Israel reportedly weigh fresh Iran strikes](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/22/oil-prices-today-wti-brent-saudi-arabia.html) — CNBC
Brent rose **2.68% to $111.74** and WTI **2.21% to $99.30** as of 6:30 a.m. ET. That puts WTI within a dollar of $100, and it comes the morning after Southwest cut its 2026 profit forecast on fuel.
Affected sectors: Energy, Airlines, Consumer

### [China's stock market sinks into bear territory as CSI 300 extends 10-week rout](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/22/chinas-stock-market-csi-300-bear-market.html) — CNBC
The CSI 300 fell **20% from its June peak**, its tenth straight weekly decline, as margin erosion from intense domestic competition keeps hitting profit growth. The Hang Seng dropped 3% and the Nikkei 2.7% in a tech-led session. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang told CNBC from GTC Berlin that neocloud demand is ["off the charts" but capital is scarce](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/22/nvidia-jensen-huang-neocloud-demand-capital.html), with CoreWeave and Nebius down about 4% pre-market as the [AI-credit worries](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/21/coreweaves-market-confidence-erodes-as-bond-yields-spike-stock-sinks.html) from last night's session carry over.
Affected sectors: Tech, Semiconductors, AI Infrastructure

### Earnings reactions reverse overnight
Meta, Tesla and IBM all beat after the close, and all three are lower this morning. Meta traded as high as **$746 (+6.8%)** in the first hour after hours on its revenue beat and $73–76 billion Q4 guide, then gave the entire gain back and sits at **$685 (-1.8%)** pre-market. [Tesla](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/21/tesla-tsla-q3-2026-earnings-report.html) beat on EPS (48 cents vs. 43 cents) and revenue but is down **3.6%** as auto revenue fell 3% and free cash flow dropped 62%. [IBM](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/21/ibm-q3-earnings-report-2026.html) beat on both lines and raised its free cash flow outlook, but a narrower-than-expected gross margin has the stock down **5.1%**.
Affected sectors: Tech, Autos, Software

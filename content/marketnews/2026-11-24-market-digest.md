## Breaking News & Macro Events

### [Nvidia falls 5.9% as the Huang comeback rally cools](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/nvidia-stock-jensen-huang-return.html) — CNBC
Nvidia closed at **$155.38**, giving back about a third of Monday's 31.5% gain. The rest of the chip group fell harder: Micron declined 10.97%, AMD 8.86%, Marvell 9.10% and Arm 8.55%. Neoclouds CoreWeave, IREN and Nebius each fell more than 10%. At his [first all-hands meeting since returning](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/jensen-huang-nvidia-employee-meeting.html), Huang apologized to staff ("I hurt all of you") and said Nvidia doesn't need a succession plan the way a normal company does. Bank of America wrote: "Reinstatement should not be confused with the end of scrutiny." Huang's closed-door House interview is set for **Dec. 1** and his public Senate Banking testimony for **Dec. 3**.

### [U.S. adds Megaspeed and six other firms to trade blacklist](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/us-adds-megaspeed-to-trade-blacklist-over-export-control-concerns-.html) — CNBC (Jenny Lee)
Late Monday, Commerce added Singapore-based Megaspeed and six other Southeast Asian companies to its trade blacklist, under a "presumption of denial" for license applications. The filing cited a "significant risk" of diverting advanced computing products. The list includes PT Megaspeed International, Malaysia's MSFusion and Megaspeed (Thailand). According to Reuters, it also includes Apex Enterprise Solutions, an Alibaba subsidiary in Singapore. Alibaba said Apex had never diverted restricted chips. Commerce and Justice said Nvidia had put Megaspeed on its "no sell" list in June 2025, a month after investigators asked for its sales records.

### [WSJ: U.S. preparing higher tariffs on dozens of countries, including China](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) — CNBC, citing the Wall Street Journal
CNBC listed the report as one of the factors behind the morning's weak sentiment. Around midday, CNBC's Megan Cassella [reported](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/11/24/new-u-s-tariffs-will-be-targeted-will-not-exceed-trade-deal-levels-source-says.html), citing a source, that the new tariffs will be targeted and will not exceed trade-deal levels. We have not seen the rates or a timeline.

### [Anthropic closes below its $83 IPO price](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/anthropic-stock-ipo-price.html) — CNBC (Ashley Capoot)
Anthropic fell **6.5%** to close below its IPO price, its third straight loss since opening at $111.50 on Thursday after raising $81.3 billion. Analysts initiating coverage are still positive: Canaccord rates it buy with a $135 target, Oppenheimer outperform with $150, and Wedbush outperform with $120. Canaccord cautioned: "The risks are unusually large, particularly around infrastructure commitments."

### [Consumer confidence jumps to 103.3 after the midterms](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/consumer-confidence-november-election-midterm.html) — CNBC (Jeff Cox)
The Conference Board index rose **11.4 points** to 103.3, far above the Dow Jones consensus of 92.2. Confidence rose sharply among Democrats and independents and fell slightly among Republicans. The jobs "plentiful" share rose to 30.8% from 26.2%. Twelve-month inflation expectations fell for a second month but remain high at **5.7%**. In rates, CME FedWatch now prices a [100% chance](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/treasury-yields-bonds-inflation.html) of a 25 bp hike on Dec. 9. A 5-year auction drew slightly soft demand.

### [Retail and pharma lead the defensive rotation](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/stocks-moving-the-most-today-live-updates.html) — CNBC
Abercrombie & Fitch rose 21%, Dick's 16% and Kohl's about 7% on earnings beats and raised guidance. Best Buy rose 3% after [6.1% comparable sales growth](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/best-buy-bby-q3-2027-earnings.html), its strongest in five years. Sanofi agreed to [buy Insmed for $41 billion](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/insmed-sanofi-deal-stock-pharma.html) ($175 a share in cash), pharma's largest deal since Pfizer–Seagen in 2023, and Insmed closed up 14.1%. Analog Devices fell 18% despite a beat, and Hewlett Packard Enterprise fell 11% despite a beat and a raised outlook. After the close, Dell [beat and guided above consensus](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/dell-earnings-report-q3-2027.html): it guided Q4 to $8.40 EPS on $56.3 billion in revenue (vs. $7.50 on $53.61 billion expected), raised its next-fiscal-year AI-server forecast to $78 billion (vs. $74 billion expected) and authorized a **$25 billion** buyback.

## Market Outlook

Monday's Huang rally ran into a split market on Tuesday. The Nasdaq fell 1.5% as Nvidia (-5.9%), Micron (-11%) and AMD (-9%) gave back gains, while the Dow added 0.85% to close above 50,000 on retail, healthcare and staples. The S&P stalled exactly at Monday's close, well short of the 7,300 level that strategists had flagged as resistance. Anthropic's slide below its $83 IPO price and new 52W lows in MSTR, CRCL, SMR and NNE suggest that the most speculative parts of the AI and crypto trades are still being sold. Consumer confidence (103.3) and the retail beats point the other way: a resilient consumer, and a market that is rotating rather than selling everything. That resilience has a cost. A December hike is now fully priced, and the 2-year yield is near 4.69%. Dell's strong guide after the close gives the AI-hardware trade a fresh test Wednesday. The bigger catalyst comes at 08:30 ET: October PCE (consensus +0.3% m/m, 3.8% y/y) and the Q3 GDP revision. A hot PCE print would lock in a hike, would raise the question of whether one is enough, and would land on a holiday-thinned tape.

## Breaking News & Macro Events

### [Consumer prices rose 0.4% in October, more than expected, complicating Fed's inflation fight](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/cpi-inflation-report-october-2026.html) — CNBC
October CPI rose **0.4%** month over month against a Dow Jones consensus of 0.3%, and core rose **0.3%** against 0.2%, per the BLS. After the release, traders fully priced a quarter-point hike at the **Dec. 8–9** FOMC meeting and put odds of a half-point move at 13.6%, up from near zero, according to CME FedWatch. The 10-year yield jumped about 12 bp to 4.98%. This is the day's dominant macro driver.

### [Fed Governor Waller sees December hike likely, opens door to possible half-point increase](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/fed-governor-waller-sees-december-hike-likely.html) — CNBC
Speaking in London before the release, Waller said he "is leaning toward" a December increase even on a soft print. On a hot one, he said, "a 50 basis point hike in the policy rate should be considered at the December meeting." He framed follow-through as a credibility issue: "Hiking only 25 basis points would put our credibility at risk, a risk I am not eager to take." Core came in at exactly the 0.3% he had flagged as the trigger.

### [Nvidia sells off for second day in fallout from Jensen Huang's affair with alleged spy](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/nvidia-nvda-jensen-huang-affair-chinese-spy.html) — CNBC
NVDA fell another 5.5% after Monday's 4%+ drop. It briefly dipped below a $4 trillion market cap intraday, and a class-action suit has been filed in San Jose. The board said it [retained Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/nvidia-wachtell-lipton-huang-affair.html), led by former acting SDNY U.S. Attorney Joon H. Kim, to investigate whether company secrets or national security were compromised. The selloff spilled into AI servers (SMCI) and drove a Nasdaq underperformance.

### [Anthropic sets IPO price range of $70 to $78, looks to start trading Nov. 19, sources say](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-sets-price-range-of-70-to-78-in-upcoming-ipo.html) — CNBC
An amended filing sets **825 million shares** at $70–$78. Pricing is planned for Nov. 18 and trading on Nov. 19, with ~20% earmarked for retail. At the top of the range, that implies up to $1.405 trillion fully diluted. AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC agreed to buy $26 billion of stock in a concurrent private placement at the IPO price. Separately, [Microsoft dropped about 3% midday](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/stock-market-movers-live-updates.html) after Anthropic launched Claude Sheets, Whiteboards and Data, which compete with Office.

### [White House vows to press ahead with tariffs despite midterm backlash over rising prices](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/trump-tariffs-midterms-gary-cohn-inflation.html) — CNBC
A week after the midterms, White House public-liaison director Jim Goyer told the B20 summit "we are just getting started" on tariffs. Former Trump economic adviser Gary Cohn countered on the same stage: "Tariffs are a consumption tax on the U.S. consumer." Coming on a hot-CPI day, the signal is that there is no policy relief on goods prices.

### [U.S. gas prices fall below $4 for the first time since July](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/us-gas-prices-below-four-dollars.html) — CNBC
The AAA national average hit $3.9788, down ~39 cents in a month. Brent briefly fell below $90 for the first time since August as Trump's comments raised hopes of an end to the nearly nine-month Iran conflict. Energy CPI cooled to +0.7% in October from +5.2% in September, so the hot headline was not an energy story.

## Market Outlook

A hotter-than-expected October CPI (+0.4% headline, +0.3% core) took Treasury yields up roughly 12 bp to 4.98%. Markets now fully price a December hike, and the odds of a half-point move have jumped. The equity reaction was contained: the S&P 500 fell 0.38%, the VIX faded from 21.61 to 19.46, and gains in UNH and TSM (on record October sales) offset some of the selling. Most of the damage came from NVDA's second straight scandal-driven drop (-5.5%) and the speculative momentum names (HIMS, nuclear, crypto) that led Monday's rally. With Governor Waller explicitly tying a 50 bp hike to a core print "around 0.3," Friday's PPI and next week's Nvidia earnings and Anthropic pricing (Nov. 17–18) form a tight cluster of catalysts. Their outcome will likely decide whether this rate repricing stays orderly.

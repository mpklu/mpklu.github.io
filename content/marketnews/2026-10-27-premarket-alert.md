## Overnight Developments

### [SoFi beats, raises 2026 guidance and introduces a 2027 outlook](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1818874/000181887426000065/a2026q3earningsrelease.htm) — SoFi via SEC EDGAR
SoFi filed its third-quarter release at **7:02 a.m. ET**, and the stock jumped in the same five-minute bar, from $14.40 to $15.31. Adjusted net revenue rose **44% to a record $1.37 billion**, adjusted EBITDA rose **70% to $470 million**, and EPS came in at **$0.18**, against the $0.17 consensus in [Seeking Alpha's preview](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4657149-sofi-q3-earnings-preview-membership-growth-and-loan-demand-in-focus). Members grew 35% to 17.1 million. The guidance moved the stock more than the quarter did. SoFi raised 2026 to **$5.04 billion** adjusted net revenue and $0.61 EPS, and introduced a 2027 outlook of **$6.4 billion** revenue and **$0.90 EPS**, which it calls "well ahead of analyst estimates." The stock was down about 45% for the year going into the print.
Affected sectors: Fintech, Banks

### [Nvidia to invest $3 billion in Meta as it raises $95 billion for AI](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/27/nvidia-invest-meta.html) — CNBC
On Monday, Meta said it would raise $35 billion in underwritten equity offerings, followed by $60 billion through an at-the-market program. It named Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom as interested in buying up to $5 billion of shares. Nvidia confirmed a **$3 billion** stake on Tuesday. As part of the deal, Meta will expand its Vera Rubin and Vera CPU deployments, and the two companies will deepen their work on Meta's Muse agent. Monday's announcement put a size on the shelf Meta had filed unsized at 6:41 a.m. that morning. CNBC reports both stocks flat pre-market. META is -0.1% and NVDA +0.8% on our prints.
Affected sectors: Tech, Semiconductors, AI Infrastructure

### [UPS beats and raises full-year guidance again](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/27/ups-ups-q3-2026-earnings.html) — CNBC
UPS earned **$1.69** adjusted EPS. It raised full-year revenue guidance to about **$92.6 billion** from $91.2 billion, its second raise in two quarters. Domestic revenue rose 4.5%, even though the company had guided to flat domestic revenue while it shrinks its Amazon volume. International revenue rose 7.1% and supply chain solutions rose 18.2%. The stock is **+1.5%** pre-market.
Affected sectors: Transports, Industrials

### [Oil inches higher as Iran diplomacy meets continued Houthi attacks](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/27/oil-today-iran-us-crude-wti-brent-trump.html) — CNBC
As of 6:23 a.m. ET, Brent was **+0.22% at $116.07** and WTI +0.52% at $102.29. Prices had fallen earlier after Monday's talks in Tehran between Saudi, Turkish, Pakistani and Iranian officials, then recovered those losses. The Houthis kept attacking Saudi Arabia over the weekend, and Trump told Fox News on Sunday he was nearing a decision on whether to make a deal with Iran or escalate.
Affected sectors: Energy, Airlines

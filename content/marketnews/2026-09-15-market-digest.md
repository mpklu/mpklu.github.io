## Breaking News & Macro Events

### [Oil surges as US–Iran strikes disrupt the Strait of Hormuz](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/9/7/oil-prices-surge-as-us-iran-strikes-intensify-in-strait-of-hormuz) — Al Jazeera

Crude was the day's dominant macro story. WTI settled near **$105.92 (+3.96%)** and Brent near **$108.64 (+2.35%)**, with Brent at four-month highs. Two supply constraints are compounding: traffic through the Strait of Hormuz has thinned to roughly 10 commodity ships per day over the last ten days amid escalating US–Iran strikes, and Saudi Arabia's East–West pipeline — the primary workaround for Hormuz flows — was shut last week with Aramco yet to give a restart timeline. [Analysts cited by ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-15/oil-could-surge-to-150-dollars-middle-east-war-intensifies/107155830) argue Brent may need to approach $150 to force demand destruction in emerging Asia if inventory depletion continues. An energy shock arriving the day before a Fed decision is an awkward coincidence of timing.

### [Treasury yields touch multi-year highs ahead of Fed decision day](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-tuesday-september-15-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-081307838.html) — Yahoo Finance

The 10-year yield pushed to **5.00% (+3.5bp)** and the 30-year to **5.36% (+3.5bp)**, both at or near their highest levels since 2007. Rising long-end yields alongside a rising oil price is the combination that pressures equity multiples from two directions at once, and it explains why the decline was led by the rate-sensitive NASDAQ rather than the Dow.

### FOMC meeting is underway — decision tomorrow at 2:00pm ET

The September FOMC meeting began **today** and concludes Wednesday, September 16, with a rate decision, a press conference, and an updated Summary of Economic Projections. The current target range is **3.50%–3.75%**, held since December 2025 (effective fed funds 3.63%). Recent reporting put market-implied odds of a 25bp *hike* in the 86–90% range following the August inflation data, which would lift the range to 3.75%–4.00% and mark the first increase since 2023. As [one preview framed it](https://cambridgecurrencies.com/next-federal-reserve-interest-rate-decision/), the dot plot may matter more than the decision: whether this is a one-and-done insurance hike or the opening of a new tightening leg is the question markets actually need answered.

### Empire State manufacturing badly misses

The NY Empire State Manufacturing Index printed **7.60** against a **14.75** consensus and a **20.60** prior — a sharp deceleration, and a data point that cuts directly against the hawkish case the Fed appears poised to act on.

## Market Outlook

Equities drifted lower across the board today as the 10-year yield touched 5.00% and the 30-year 5.36%, both at levels unseen since 2007, while WTI crude jumped nearly 4% on disrupted Strait of Hormuz traffic and the continued shutdown of Saudi Arabia's East–West pipeline. The combination matters more than either piece alone: an energy-driven inflation impulse arriving the day before an FOMC decision that markets have already positioned for as a hike leaves the Fed defending a tightening move into a manufacturing sector that just delivered a sharp Empire State miss at 7.60 against a 14.75 consensus. That tension showed up beneath the surface today — the crypto complex broke hard, with Bitcoin off 3.2% dragging Circle down 11.4% and Strategy 5.4%, while Tempus AI's 10.7% gain showed capital still rotating toward idiosyncratic growth stories rather than leaving risk assets entirely. A flat gold price against a genuine geopolitical supply shock suggests real yields, not fear, are setting the tone. Tomorrow's dot plot warrants far more attention than the rate decision itself, since whether this is an insurance hike or the first step of a new tightening cycle will determine how much further the long end has to run — with quadruple witching on Friday likely to amplify whatever direction that answer sets.

## Overnight Developments

### [Micron's blowout quarter ignites an AI chip rally in Asia](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000723125/000072312526000018/mu-20260930.htm) — SEC / Micron 8-K

Micron reported fiscal Q4 after Tuesday's close: revenue of **$54.2 billion**, up 379% year-over-year, with adjusted EPS of **$33.42** and gross margin near 87%. Full-year revenue hit a record **$133.2 billion**. The read-through on AI memory demand sent Japanese semiconductor names sharply higher overnight — Advantest, Tokyo Electron and SoftBank led the Nikkei to its biggest move in weeks.

Affected sectors: Semiconductors, Technology, AI infrastructure

**The catch worth watching:** Micron's own stock has not followed. MU opened pre-market at $1,072.50 and faded steadily to roughly **$1,065 — essentially flat** against Tuesday's close. Asia bought the AI theme far harder than US traders have so far bid the company that produced it. That divergence is the single most important thing to watch at the open.

### Rates remain the headwind

The US 10-year yield closed Tuesday at **5.29%**, extending a steady climb (5.18% → 5.24% → 5.26% → 5.29% over four sessions). The 30-year sits at 5.64%. This is a continuing grind rather than an overnight shock, but it caps how much multiple expansion the AI trade can get.

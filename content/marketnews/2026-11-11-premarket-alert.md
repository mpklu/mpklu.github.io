## Overnight Developments

### [CoreWeave beats on revenue and lifts guidance, easing AI slowdown concerns](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/11/coreweave-crwv-outlook-earnings-ai.html) — CNBC
CoreWeave reported Q3 revenue up **168% to $3.7 billion** after Tuesday's close. It raised its 2026 outlook to **$13.0–13.3 billion in revenue** and **$1.275–1.375 billion in adjusted operating income**, above the LSEG estimate of $12.86 billion in revenue and the Visible Alpha estimate of $1.07 billion in operating income. CoreWeave shares were up double digits before the open, and Nebius and Iren also rose. The report eased doubts about the AI build-out, and that lifted chip stocks around the world. [CNBC's live blog](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) reports South Korea's Kospi closed **+2.6%** (SK Hynix +5.4%), and in Europe Infineon rose 6.3%, ASMI 5.5% and ASML about 3%.
Affected sectors: Semiconductors, AI infrastructure, Tech

### [Nvidia shares rebound as Huang faces scrutiny ahead of earnings](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/11/nvidia-stock-jensen-huang-affair-china.html) — CNBC
Nvidia is bouncing after falling **4.3% Monday** and **5.47% Tuesday** on Jensen Huang's affair with an alleged Chinese spy. The company has hired Wachtell Lipton to investigate whether company secrets or national security were compromised. Morgan Stanley dropped NVDA as a top pick on Wednesday, and earnings are due **Nov. 17**.
Affected sectors: Semiconductors, Tech

### Background: rate-hike odds and Iran
Tuesday's October CPI came in at **+0.4% m/m**, above expectations. After the release, CME FedWatch priced a December quarter-point **hike** at about **90%**, according to the live blog. The **bond market is closed today for Veterans Day**, but stocks trade normally. Separately, Treasury Secretary Bessent was [reportedly moved to a military base over Iranian threats on his life](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/11/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-bessent-threats.html). This adds to the existing US-Iran conflict but is not a new escalation, and WTI is little changed near **$86.6**.

## Breaking News & Macro Events

### [Brent crude tops $100 as U.S.-Iran tit-for-tat strikes stoke oil supply worries](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/09/oil-prices-today-wti-brent-us-iran-hormuz-attacks.html) — CNBC

Brent crossed $100 a barrel for the first time since July after U.S. forces destroyed five Iranian crude tankers Tuesday night in retaliation for an attempted attack on an American warship, and Iran responded by striking ten ships in the Strait of Hormuz. The waterway carries roughly a fifth of global seaborne oil, and [transits have collapsed](https://www.wbur.org/hereandnow/2026/09/09/oil-prices-strait-of-hormuz) from around 130 vessels a day to a low double-digit trickle. This is a supply shock, not a demand story — which is why equities fell while energy names and crude both rallied.

### [Canada's retaliatory tariffs worth $27.6 billion take effect as trade rift deepens](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/canada-retaliatory-tariffs.html) — CNBC

Canada's counter-tariffs on $27.6B of U.S. goods took effect at 12:01 a.m. Tuesday, with rates from 15% to 50% across steel, aluminum, dairy, appliances, agricultural equipment and electronics. Ottawa framed it as a dollar-for-dollar answer to the 50% U.S. Section 338 tariffs imposed on roughly $20B of Canadian exports last month. The second-order effect that matters for markets is goods-price pass-through landing in the same CPI prints the Fed is about to react to.

### [September Fed decision is a coin flip as rate hike odds increase post-Warsh](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/28/-september-fed-decision-now-a-coin-flip-as-rate-hike-odds-increase.html) — CNBC

Markets now price meaningful odds of a 25bp **hike** at next week's FOMC — CME FedWatch is near 56%, with Kalshi and Polymarket closer to 48-49%. The repricing followed Chair Kevin Warsh's Jackson Hole remarks arguing that a better-than-expected summer of inflation data did not prove the underlying trend had improved. Today's oil move pushes in the same direction.

## Market Outlook

Equities sold off broadly — S&P 500 -0.48%, Dow -0.77%, and a notably weaker Russell 2000 at -1.32% — as Brent vaulted past $100 on U.S.-Iran strikes in the Strait of Hormuz and the 10-year Treasury yield closed at a 52-week high of 4.84%. An energy supply shock landing six days before an FOMC meeting where markets already price roughly even odds of a rate *hike* is an uncomfortable combination: it lifts headline inflation exactly when Chair Warsh has signaled he wants to re-establish credibility on the 2% target, and the small-cap underperformance suggests investors are repricing rate-sensitive balance sheets rather than trimming growth exposure. That Meta could still add 6.6% on the Muse launch shows idiosyncratic AI catalysts continue to cut through a hostile macro tape, which warrants attention for anyone assuming correlations have gone to one. Friday's August CPI is now the most consequential print of the quarter — it lands four days before the decision, and a hot headline number would make a September 16 hike close to settled.

## Overnight Developments

### [Fed's Waller says a half-point December hike "should be considered"](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/fed-governor-waller-sees-december-hike-likely.html) — CNBC
Speaking in London on Tuesday, Fed Governor Christopher Waller said he "is leaning toward" a December rate increase even if October inflation comes in softer than expected. If readings come in hotter, he would back "more robust action": "In that case, I believe a 50 basis point hike in the policy rate should be considered at the December meeting." He added that "hiking only 25 basis points would put our credibility at risk." This raises the stakes on this morning's CPI at 8:30 a.m. ET, one of the last inflation reports before the Dec. 8-9 FOMC. The [Dow Jones consensus](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/09/october-cpi-report-2026-inflation.html) is **+0.3% headline** and **+0.2% core** month over month, after September's 0.6% and 0.3%. The 10-year yield ended Monday at **4.86%**.
Affected sectors: Rate-sensitive growth, Financials, Real estate, Broad market

### [Brent dips below $90 for the first time since August](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/oil-prices-trump-iran.html) — CNBC
Crude extended Monday's slide after [Trump said the Iran war could end "fairly soon"](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/trump-iran-war-hormuz-.html) and called new strikes "quite unlikely." He also said Iran was "talking very politely" with Washington, while keeping the naval blockade in place and the option to resume strikes open. Brent's January contract traded below $90 early in the session. On our bars it is now at about **$90.6 (-2.1%)**, with December WTI at about **$85.4 (-1.6%)**, both against Monday's closes.
Affected sectors: Energy, Airlines, Transports

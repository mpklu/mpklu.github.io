## Breaking News & Macro Events

### [Nvidia stock suffers longest weekly losing streak since 2012 as market value sinks by $1.7 trillion](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/nvidia-stock-jensen-affair-ai.html) — CNBC
NVDA fell another **2.0%** today and is down 13% this week. That is its sixth straight weekly decline, a roughly 30% slide over the streak, and it has now slipped below Apple in market value. Interim CEO **Colette Kress** runs the company while the board searches inside and outside for a permanent successor. HSBC [downgraded the stock to hold and cut its target to $170 from $365](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/hsbc-downgrades-nvidia-says-stock-to-struggle-until-new-ceo-named.html). Analyst Frank Lee argued that "uncertainties will prevail until a permanent succession plan is laid out," and he flagged tighter export curbs as a real risk to FY2028 EPS. Shares were down as much as 4.7% intraday before buyers trimmed the loss.

### [Wholesale prices rose 0.3% in October, putting annual rate at 6.5%, the highest in nearly four years](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/ppi-inflation-report-october-2026.html) — CNBC
Headline PPI rose **0.3%** against a 0.4% consensus, and core PPI rose 0.3%, in line. The annual rate climbed to **6.5%**, the highest since November 2022. CNBC says markets are fully pricing a quarter-point **hike** at the Dec. 8–9 FOMC, with slight odds of a half-point. Chicago Fed President Austan Goolsbee [told CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/chicago-feds-goolsbee-says-central-bank-has-definitely-got-work-to-do-on-inflation.html) the Fed "has definitely got work to do to continue to reestablish its credibility." The 10-year yield rose 5 bp to **5.15%**. Mortgage REITs (REM) hit their lowest level since April 2020.

### [Bessent blames Lutnick's Commerce as he slams Nvidia chip 'leakage' to China](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/us-inflation-outlook-federal-reserve-rate-hike.html) — CNBC
Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said Treasury, Defense, State and USTR "were in favor of putting Megaspeed on the entity list for months." Megaspeed is the company at the center of the Huang scandal. Bessent said Commerce is responsible, adding that "any leakage is very frustrating." An open cabinet split over export controls raises the odds of tighter China rules, which is the risk HSBC flagged for NVDA. It also bears on TSM and the AI-server names.

### [Stocks making the biggest moves Friday: Nvidia, Cisco Systems, AMD, Apple, Modine & more](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/stocks-moving-the-most-today-live-updates.html) — CNBC
The AI-infrastructure trade broke down again. **Dell, AMD, Nebius and CoreWeave** each fell around 10%, Coherent lost more than 9% and Sandisk 8%. Broadcom fell 5% after a Rothschild & Co. Redburn downgrade. Cisco dropped 6% even after beating estimates and raising guidance. Money rotated into perceived safe havens instead: **Apple** set an intraday record, and Costco hit a 52-week high.

### [AI risks are the biggest threat to the financial system, Fed survey finds](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/ai-risks-financial-system-federal-reserve.html) — CNBC
The Fed's twice-yearly financial stability survey, released after the close, put AI well ahead of every other risk to the financial system. Respondents' AI worries spanned debt, stock valuations and security. They singled out elevated equity valuations, which could correct if earnings disappoint, and cybersecurity risk.

### [FAA issues nationwide ground stop citing communication issues](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/faa-ground-stop.html) — CNBC
After the close, the FAA halted departures at all U.S. airports. It cited communication problems affecting multiple Air Route Traffic Control Centers, and technicians are investigating. If the outage runs long, watch the airline stocks and UPS on Monday.

## Market Outlook

The week ended with a split tape. The Dow edged up 0.1% while the Nasdaq fell 0.9% as Nvidia's leadership crisis pulled Dell, AMD and CoreWeave down roughly 10%. Even so, all three averages posted their best week since early August. Money is rotating, not leaving: Apple set a record and Costco a 52-week high while the AI-infrastructure complex was sold. That suggests investors are paying up for earnings certainty over AI capex exposure. The macro backdrop leaves little room for error. PPI is running 6.5% year over year, the 10-year is at 5.15%, and markets fully price a December hike, so this is not a Fed that will cushion a growth scare. Tuesday's Nvidia earnings, the first under interim CEO Colette Kress, are the week's key catalyst. Guidance and any read on China export exposure will decide whether the AI trade stabilizes or the rotation deepens.

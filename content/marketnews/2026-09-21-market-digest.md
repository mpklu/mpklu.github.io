## Breaking News & Macro Events

### [Nasdaq, S&P 500 surge as oil retreats on Iran diplomacy hopes](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-today-sept-21-135426827.html) — Yahoo Finance
Stocks opened the week with their strongest session since early August as crude extended its slide: front-month WTI fell **4.2% to $92.03** and Brent held below $100 for a fourth straight session. The bid came from geopolitics rather than earnings. Trump signaled he is willing to meet Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian during this week's UN General Assembly, and analysts framed the move as the market removing some of the geopolitical premium built into crude while Hormuz shipping recovers. The 10-year Treasury yield slipped back under 5% as traders trimmed bets on an accelerated Fed hiking path. The rally was concentrated: Technology and Communication Services led while Energy fell nearly 2%, and the Russell 2000 lagged the Nasdaq by 1.7 points.

### [Trump–Xi summit set for this week as tariff truce nears November expiry](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/world/2026-09-21-how-trump-and-xi-went-from-tariff-war-to-trade-truce/) — TimesLive / Reuters
Xi Jinping meets Trump in Washington on **Thursday, September 24**, with tariffs, critical minerals, and artificial intelligence on the agenda. Treasury Secretary Bessent met Vice Premier He Lifeng ahead of the summit and floated a new US–China AI dialogue. The stakes are the trade truce that expires in November; the US layered 12.5% tariffs on 60 trading partners in July, and semiconductors and AI infrastructure are the most direct beneficiaries of any extension. Today's outsized moves in INTC and TSM reflect that positioning.

**Fed context:** the FOMC [raised rates 25bp to 3.75%–4.00% on September 16](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/16/fed-rate-decision-september-2026.html), the first hike since 2023, with 16 of 18 officials projecting at least one more increase this year. Today's rally is happening five sessions after that hike, built on oil-driven inflation relief rather than on any softening from the Fed.

## Market Outlook

Monday delivered the broadest risk-on session in six weeks, with the Nasdaq up 2.26% and the S&P 500 up 1.49%, as WTI fell 4.2% to $92 on Iran diplomacy hopes and the 10-year slipped back below 5%. The uncomfortable part is what the rally is built on: this is oil-driven inflation relief arriving five sessions after the Fed hiked to 3.75%–4.00% with more hikes penciled in, so the floor under equities is a commodity price, not a policy pivot. Leadership was also narrow. META and INTC each gained more than 11% on company-specific catalysts while the Russell 2000 added barely half a percent, and NVDA and AAPL now sit within 4% of their 52-week highs, which leaves little cushion if crude reverses. Watch Thursday's Trump–Xi summit and Costco's report on the same evening, with Wednesday's flash PMIs as the first read on whether September's tightening is showing up in activity; a rebound in WTI back toward $100 would test how much of today's move was conviction versus relief.

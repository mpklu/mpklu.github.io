## Breaking News & Macro Events

### [US and Iran Pause Military Strikes; Oil Tumbles](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-26/oil-tumbles-as-us-and-iran-pause-military-strikes-markets-wrap) — Bloomberg
A fragile memorandum of understanding paused US–Iran military strikes, triggering a relief
rally across risk assets and collapsing the geopolitical premium in crude. WTI fell **3.91%**
to $81.88 and Brent dropped **5.63%** to $87.64 — the single largest input into today's
cross-asset move. The Strait of Hormuz remains a live vulnerability, so the de-escalation
is best read as a pause rather than a resolution.

### [Chip Stocks Sell Off Hard on Leverage and Chinese Competition](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/why-is-nvidia-stock-sliding-today-93CH-4814453) — Investing.com
NVIDIA fell **4.98%** to $196.51, breaking back below $200, with AMD off roughly 8% and Intel
down intraday. The move was driven by a combination of competition from cheaper Chinese AI
models, questions about the sustainability of AI capex financing, and forced selling from
leveraged positions. Notably this happened while the broader tape was positive — a
sector-specific unwind, not a market-wide risk-off event.

### [China Says US Pledged to Cap Replacement Tariffs at 20%](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/china-says-us-pledged-to-cap-replacement-tariffs-at-20) — Bloomberg
China's Commerce Ministry disclosed that bilateral trade talks produced a US commitment to
cap replacement tariffs on Chinese goods at 20%. With the current effective level near 12.5%,
that leaves roughly 7.5 points of headroom — a ceiling, but one well above today's rate.

### [Trump Imposes 50% Tariffs on Additional Canadian Goods](https://ourtake.bakerbotts.com/post/102ndeb/trump-tariff-tracker-july-21-2026) — Baker Botts
Proclamations signed July 20 under Section 338 impose an additional 50% tariff on Canadian
wine, beverages, hockey sticks, and cement, while exempting energy and critical minerals.
The carve-outs limit the macro impact but the escalation pattern warrants attention.

### [Section 301 Investigation Opened Against the EU](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/24/economy/trump-tariffs-whats-next) — CNN Business
The administration launched a Section 301 investigation into the EU over alleged
discriminatory treatment of large US technology companies including Google, Apple, Meta,
and Amazon — a direct regulatory overhang for four of the largest index constituents.

## Market Outlook

Headline indices went almost nowhere today, but that flat close concealed a genuine rotation:
the Dow added 0.51% and the Russell 2000 0.62% while the Nasdaq fell 0.18%, as a leveraged
unwind hit semiconductors — NVIDIA down 4.98%, AMD off roughly 8% — even as Apple closed above
its 52-week high. The proximate causes were external rather than fundamental: a US–Iran strike
pause collapsed the crude premium (Brent -5.63%), and cheap Chinese AI models plus questions
about AI capex financing gave margined holders a reason to reduce, all while TSMC's 77%
profit growth and Intel's doubled EPS sat unrewarded. That divergence between results and
multiples is the tell — the market is repricing what it will pay for AI capex, not whether the
demand is real, and Alphabet's negative $5.86B quarterly free cash flow on $195–205B of
guided spend is the reason why. Falling energy prices and June CPI at 3.5% give the Fed room
to hold comfortably tomorrow, but with core at 2.6% and the September debate reopening, the
statement's language will matter more than the decision. The next 72 hours carry the load:
the FOMC concludes Wednesday alongside Meta and SoFi earnings, then Apple, Amazon, Strategy,
and Tempus all report Thursday — a capex referendum and a rate decision landing in the same
week warrants close attention.

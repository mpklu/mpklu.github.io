## Breaking News & Macro Events

### [July CPI cools to 3.4% annual, headline up 0.1% on the month](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-wednesday-august-12-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-cpi-report-091555133.html) — Yahoo Finance / BLS

The July print came in at **+0.1% month-over-month** and **3.4% year-over-year**,
with core CPI at **+0.2%** and **2.5%** annually. Both headline and core ticked
down from June. The important caveat: this data covers July, which means it
predates the August energy shock entirely — the Strait of Hormuz closure that
added roughly 7% to crude in the first half of this month will not show up until
the August report lands in September. Markets treated the print as removing
near-term pressure for another hike; odds of the Fed holding at the September
meeting rose on the release.

### [Intel prices upsized $20 billion common stock offering at $95](https://www.intc.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1779/intel-announces-upsize-and-pricing-of-20-billion-common) — Intel Investor Relations

Intel priced 210,526,315 shares at $95, upsizing from the $15 billion originally
proposed and netting approximately $19.7 billion. The deal closed today, and the
stock finished at $100.95 — **6.3% above the offer price** and up 3.3% on the
session. A dilutive raise of this size that leaves the stock well through the
strike is the market telling you it wants Intel to have the capital more than it
minds the dilution. Proceeds are earmarked for general corporate purposes
including capital expenditures.

### [Nvidia signs MOUs with six firms to mobilize $500 billion for AI infrastructure](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/08/61148559/whats-going-on-with-nvidia-stock-on-wednesday-3) — Benzinga

Confirmed August 10: memoranda of understanding with Apollo, BlackRock,
Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR to mobilize more than $500 billion
of third-party capital for AI infrastructure over time. Combined with Supermicro's
guidance tonight, this is the second data point this week arguing that the funding
layer beneath AI capex is deepening rather than tightening.

## Market Outlook

A benign July CPI — 0.1% monthly, 3.4% annual, with core easing to 2.5% — let
equities grind higher on improving breadth, and Supermicro's guidance shock did
the rest, pulling NVDA, TSM and INTC along with it while the VIX broke to 14.55.
The more useful read is what did *not* happen: breakevens fell two basis points
and the 10-year sat still, meaning the bond market extracted no new information
from the print and the September FOMC remains genuinely contested, with at least
one voter having already dissented in favor of a hike. Investors should hold that
disinflation read loosely — July's data predates August's energy shock entirely,
and the roughly 7% crude move of the past two weeks will not appear until the
September release. Meanwhile the AI-infrastructure complex is re-rating on
funding and order-book evidence rather than sentiment, which is a sturdier
foundation than the one it stood on in the spring, though it raises the bar for
what counts as a beat. The next two sessions bring PPI and retail sales, and the
setup into Nvidia's August 26 print now carries materially higher expectations
than it did a week ago.

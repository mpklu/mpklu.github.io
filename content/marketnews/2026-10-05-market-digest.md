## Breaking News & Macro Events

### [Intel Slides as TSMC Explores Terafab Tie-Up](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/intel-stock-slides-as-tsmc-explores-terafab-tieup-analyst-flags-share-losses-4931558) — Investing.com

The story that set up this morning's gap played out through the close, and it was the cleanest trade on the board. TSMC is reportedly exploring a partnership on Elon Musk's **Terafab** project in Texas — the venture where **Intel had been the sole named manufacturing partner** since April. The proposed structure would hand TSMC ownership and operation of the facility, with Terafab as anchor customer. Musk characterized the talks as early: *"Just discussions, but something may come of it."*

The same report carried a second blow: Susquehanna estimates Intel continued losing CPU share in Q3, with laptop share down **3.9% q/q to 68.1%** against AMD's 3.1% gain, and desktop share falling to roughly **76%** — the lowest in the firm's study since Q2 2023.

The market priced it as a direct transfer. **INTC closed −2.63%** (recovering meaningfully from −4.26% in pre-market) while **TSM gained +2.75%**. Worth noting what did *not* happen: the broader semiconductor complex barely moved, with the PHLX Semiconductor Index up just **+0.27%** and **AMD down 0.34%** despite being the named beneficiary of the share-shift data. This was repriced as a company-specific foundry story, not a sector call.

### ISM Services Slips Below Expectations

The September ISM Services PMI, released this morning, came in at **54.9** against a consensus near **55.0** — a marginal miss that kept the index comfortably in expansion territory. It was not enough to move the rate narrative on its own, and markets largely looked through it.

## Market Outlook

Equities ground higher on a thin news day, with the NASDAQ's **+1.05%** lapping the Dow's **+0.18%** on the back of large-cap tech and a single clean semiconductor rotation — TSMC's reported move on Intel's Terafab role sent **TSM +2.75%** and **INTC −2.63%** without disturbing the broader chip complex, which closed up just 0.27%. That narrowness is the day's real signal: this was idiosyncratic repositioning rather than a broad risk-on impulse, and a VIX still pinned near 15.5 suggests the market sees nothing on the near horizon worth hedging. Beneath the index-level calm, though, the dispersion is widening — Tempus AI ran **+9%** with no identifiable catalyst, the crypto-linked names rallied against a falling Bitcoin, and the small-modular-nuclear complex (OKLO, SMR, NNE) continues to grind near 52-week lows, all of which warrants attention from anyone assuming the tape is as quiet as the headline numbers imply. With September CPI pushed out to **October 14** and the FOMC still 22 days away, this week offers little macro to trade. Watch **Wednesday's FOMC minutes** for any shift in the committee's tone, and then **UnitedHealth on October 13 and TSMC on October 15** — the first watchlist earnings of the season, and the point at which this market stops drifting on narrative and starts repricing on numbers.

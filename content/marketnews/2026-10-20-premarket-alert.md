## Overnight Developments

### [U.S. oil surges to top $107, Brent crude at five-month high amid Mideast tensions](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/20/oil-price-wti-brent-crude.html) — CNBC

Brent crude for December delivery is trading **1.9% higher at $111.42**, its highest price since May 19. Front-month WTI jumped **4.2% to around $107.50** as of 6:20 a.m. ET. November WTI is on track for its **ninth consecutive session of gains** and a monthly rise of almost 20%.

One important caveat on that WTI number: the November contract **expires today**, and CNBC attributes part of the move to expiry alongside the Middle East tensions. December WTI — the contract that matters from tomorrow — is up a more modest **2.3% to $100.83**. Read the $107 headline as a front-month squeeze on top of a real geopolitical bid, not as a clean 4% repricing of crude.

Affected sectors: Energy, Airlines, Transports, Consumer

### [Saudi Arabia postpones big investor conference to 2027 as Iran-backed Houthis escalate attacks](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/19/saudi-arabia-postpones-fii-investor-conference-to-2027.html) — CNBC

Saudi Arabia has postponed the Future Investment Initiative, originally scheduled for Riyadh from **Oct. 26 to Oct. 29**, until 2027. The decision follows a Houthi attack on **King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh on Oct. 10** that left at least **12 people dead and more than 300 injured**. Jamie Dimon, David Solomon, Stephen Schwarzman, Larry Fink and Jane Fraser had all been listed as speakers.

This is the signal worth weighing. A kingdom that has spent a decade courting global capital just decided its marquee event is not safe to hold — and some attendees had reportedly planned to fly into Bahrain and drive five hours to Riyadh rather than land there. That is a risk premium the oil market is now pricing directly.

Affected sectors: Energy, Defense, EM, Financials

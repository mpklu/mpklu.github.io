## Overnight Developments

### [Nvidia beats and raises in its first report since Huang's resignation](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/17/nvidia-nvda-earnings-report-q3-2027.html) — CNBC
Nvidia reported after Tuesday's close. Third-quarter revenue was **$115.84 billion**, against a **$109.10 billion** LSEG consensus. Fourth-quarter guidance of about **$130 billion** topped the **$125.1 billion** estimate. Interim CEO Colette Kress raised the fiscal-2028 growth forecast to **75%** from 70%. Board chair Harvey Jones said on the call that the board does not intend to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO. Nvidia is still assuming no China data-center compute revenue in its outlook.

[The shares rallied](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/nvidia-stock-earnings.html), and neoclouds CoreWeave, Nebius and Iren gained 6–10% in extended trading. Nvidia was up almost **4%** around 5 a.m. ET, but the gain had faded to about **+2.6%** by 07:00. The setup is a relief bounce. The S&P 500 fell for a fourth straight day on Tuesday (**−1.0%**, its lowest close since July 29), and the Dow dropped **1.6%**.
Affected sectors: Semiconductors, AI infrastructure, Tech

### [Iran says it targeted U.S. sites at a Kuwait air base](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/trump-iran-war-hormuz-oil-.html) — CNBC
Iran said its army targeted U.S. drone and missile installations at Kuwait's Ali Al Salem air base. Bahrain intercepted another wave of Iranian drones overnight. Oil has not priced in an escalation. WTI settled **1.2%** lower Tuesday at about **$84.9**, helped by signs of recovering flows through Hormuz. Separately, Trump [landed in China](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/trump-xi-putin-china-apec.html) for a meeting with Xi and Putin.
Affected sectors: Energy, Defense

## Overnight Developments

### [Stock futures decline as Nvidia pulls back, giving back part of Monday's rally](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) — CNBC
Monday was the best session for the S&P 500 (**+4.7%**) and Nasdaq Composite (**+6.5%**) since April 9, 2025. Nvidia jumped about 31% after the board reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO. Futures are now giving part of that back, led by the chip stocks. [Nvidia was down 5.4% pre-market as of 5:44 a.m. ET](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/nvidia-stock-jensen-huang-return.html), and Micron, Marvell, AMD and Arm were each down more than 3%. Scrutiny of Nvidia continues. Huang is due to testify before the Senate Banking Committee and to face questions from the House Foreign Affairs Committee next week, and Bank of America wrote that "reinstatement should not be confused with the end of scrutiny." CNBC also reports that Anthropic shares fell more than 4%, briefly trading below their IPO price.
Affected sectors: Semiconductors, AI infrastructure, mega-cap tech

### [U.S. adds Megaspeed to trade blacklist over export control concerns](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/us-adds-megaspeed-to-trade-blacklist-over-export-control-concerns-.html) — CNBC
Commerce added Singapore-based Megaspeed and six other Southeast Asian entities to the Entity List, with license applications reviewed under a "presumption of denial." The list includes Megaspeed affiliates in Indonesia, Malaysia and Thailand. Reuters reports that an Alibaba subsidiary in Singapore was also added. The move ties directly to the Nvidia story: former Megaspeed CEO Alice Huang is under U.S. investigation. It keeps the question of export-control diversion in the headlines.
Affected sectors: Semiconductors, AI infrastructure

### [Iran says three tankers hit mines in Hormuz despite U.S. escort](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/iran-trump-hormuz-crude-.html) — CNBC
Iran's IRGC says three oil tankers caught fire after hitting naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz while under U.S. escort. On Monday, Treasury added sanctions on more than two dozen Iranian exchange houses and front companies. Crude is not reacting so far: WTI is flat near **$80** after Monday's 4.5% drop.
Affected sectors: Energy, shipping, defense

### [Fed's Williams: more work to do on inflation](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/john-williams-fed-inflation-rate-hikes.html) — CNBC
After Monday's close, New York Fed President John Williams said the Fed "must take actions over time" to bring inflation back to 2%. This was the last major Fed remark before the Dec. 8–9 FOMC meeting. Markets fully price a quarter-point **hike** from the current 3.75%–4% range.
Affected sectors: Rate-sensitive growth, financials

## Overnight Developments

### [Bitcoin tops $79,000 as another $1B of shorts gets wiped out](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/08/21/bitcoin-ether-and-solana-climb-as-another-usd1-billion-shorts-get-wiped-out) — CoinDesk

Bitcoin ran to **$79,241** overnight — its highest since June — before easing back to roughly **$77,650**, up **6.3%** from Thursday's close and about **20% on the week**. The move is mechanical as much as fundamental: short liquidations hit roughly **$3 billion over 24 hours** against just $264 million on the long side, the largest short-liquidation event since at least 2021 per CoinGlass. Spot ETF inflows accelerated for a second day.

Affected sectors: Crypto, Fintech, Risk assets

### [Treasury buyback expansion revives the dollar-debasement trade](https://cryptobriefing.com/treasury-buyback-expansion-sparks-dollar-debasement-concerns-boosts-gold-bitcoin/) — Crypto Briefing

The proximate catalyst is the **August 19** Treasury decision to at least double liquidity-support buybacks of 10- to 30-year paper, from $2 billion to **$4 billion minimum per operation**. That came a day after the 30-year yield touched its highest level since 2007. The buyback pulled long yields back, and the "debasement" bid has broadened well beyond crypto: **gold +2.9%**, **silver +2.2%**, and **copper +2.0%** overnight, with the dollar index down 0.26%.

Affected sectors: Precious metals, Miners, Crypto, Rate-sensitives

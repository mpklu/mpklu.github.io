## Overnight Developments

### [Nvidia board opens a probe into Jensen Huang's tie to an alleged Chinese spy](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/08/nvidia-jensen-huang-investigation-china-spy.html) — CNBC
On Sunday, Nvidia's board opened an independent investigation into CEO Jensen Huang's relationship with Alice Huang, an executive at the Nvidia customer Megaspeed. Citing The New York Times and the Financial Times, CNBC reports that U.S. intelligence agencies say Alice Huang has been passing information to Beijing. Megaspeed has also been accused of helping restricted Chinese companies get Nvidia AI chips. In an email to staff, Huang admitted the affair ("I was unfaithful to my wife... I am deeply sorry"). The board says it has "no reason to believe that either national security or NVIDIA secrets were compromised," and Nvidia says Huang will stay on as CEO without interruption. [China's Foreign Ministry called the spy narrative unsubstantiated](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/09/nvidia-jensen-huang-chinese-spy-investigation.html), and [Huang faces bipartisan scrutiny in Congress](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/08/jensen-huang-nvidia-china-spy-congress.html). **NVDA is down about 5% pre-market** even though the broader tape is higher. Dan Ives told CNBC the market's worry is "does Jensen stay on as CEO?", adding that "anything could happen, and that creates a huge uncertainty."
Affected sectors: Semiconductors, AI infrastructure

### [Oil drops about 3% after Trump calls more Iran strikes "highly unlikely"](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/09/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz.html) — CNBC
On Sunday, Trump said further strikes were "highly unlikely" if talks progress on Iran's nuclear program and on reopening the Strait of Hormuz. Crude sold off on the remark, even though Rubio later called Tehran's demands "outrageous and ridiculous" and said "I wouldn't say we're on the verge of a breakthrough." Our December contract bars have **WTI at about $88.1 (-3.0%)** and **Brent at about $94.7 (-3.7%)**, both against Friday's closes. Lower oil is the main tailwind behind the [Dow futures rally of about 500 points](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/08/stock-market-today-live-updates.html).
Affected sectors: Energy, Airlines, Transports, Broad market

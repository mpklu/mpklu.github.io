## Breaking News & Macro Events

### [S&P 500 Notches Record High After Soft Inflation Data](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-thursday-august-13-sp-500-record-high-nasdaq-dow-inflation-100145282.html) — Yahoo Finance
July CPI came in cooler than expected, and the market's reaction says everything about where the current policy debate sits: money markets cut the implied odds of a September rate **hike** to roughly 35%, down from about 50% earlier in the week. This is not a rate-cut trade. Three FOMC members dissented in favor of a hike at the July meeting, so a tame inflation print relieves pressure in the direction that actually matters right now. Falling oil prices reinforced the disinflation read.

### [Silver Lake in Talks to Buy Workday](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/exclusivesilver-lake-in-talks-to-buy-workday-sources-say-4858969) — Reuters (exclusive)
Private equity firm Silver Lake is in discussions to acquire Workday, which carried a market value near $43 billion before the report. Workday shares closed **+17.8%** — their best day since 2016 — after being halted intraday, lifting market value to roughly $51 billion. Talks are ongoing with no assurance of a deal. The setup matters beyond the single name: Workday entered the day down about 15% year-to-date and more than 40% below its 2024 peak, as investors question the durability of traditional enterprise software against rapidly advancing AI. A buyout at this scale would mark the point where private capital starts treating that de-rating as an opportunity.

### [Intel Prices Upsized $20 Billion Stock Offering](https://newsroom.intel.com/corporate/intel-announces-upsize-and-pricing-of-20-billion-common-stock-offering) — Intel Newsroom
Intel upsized its offering from $15 billion to $20 billion, pricing 210,526,315 shares at $95.00 for net proceeds of about $19.7 billion, with a 30-day underwriter option on 31,578,947 additional shares. The deal reportedly drew more than $100 billion in demand and closed August 12. Proceeds are earmarked for general corporate purposes including capital expenditures and working capital — in practice, the AI foundry buildout. That a turnaround story can raise $20 billion at a discount to spot and still trade higher afterward says more about appetite for AI capacity than about Intel's execution to date.

## Notable Voices

### [Jamie Dimon on Dollar Dominance and Military Power](https://fortune.com/2026/08/11/jamie-dimon-dollar-dominance-military/) — Jamie Dimon, Fortune (2026-08-11)
The JPMorgan Chase CEO argued that the dollar's reserve currency status rests on the United States sustaining both military and economic primacy over the next 25 years, and that losing either would eventually cost it that status and produce a more fragmented, more dangerous world. It is a structural argument rather than a market call, but it lands in the same week the market is debating whether the Fed's next move is a hike — a reminder that reserve-currency privilege is what lets the US run this policy mix at all.

## Market Outlook

The S&P 500 closed at a record 7,798.99 and printed above 7,800 intraday for the first time, after a cooler-than-expected July CPI cut implied odds of a September rate **hike** to roughly 35% from about 50% earlier this week. The direction of that repricing is the story: with three FOMC members having dissented toward a hike in July, disinflation is currently relieving pressure from the tightening side rather than pulling forward cuts, and a 4-basis-point decline in the 10-year alongside a 2.6% drop in WTI gave equities the cover to extend. Breadth warrants caution, though — the Dow added just 0.13% and the Russell 2000 0.24% while the NASDAQ's 0.81% leaned on Meta and AI infrastructure, and the NASDAQ itself remains 1.1% below its June high, so this record is being set by a narrow cohort. Supermicro's $60B order book set against Q1 gross margin guidance of 10.4–10.8% captures the tension underneath the AI trade cleanly: volume is abundant, profitability per unit is not, and that question gets its most important test when NVIDIA reports in 13 days. Watch Friday's July retail sales print and next Wednesday's FOMC minutes for whether the hike-risk repricing has any durability behind it.

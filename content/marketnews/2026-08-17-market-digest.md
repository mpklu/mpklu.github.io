## Breaking News & Macro Events

### [Oil rises and the 30-year yield hits its highest level in decades as US-Iran tensions resurface](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-monday-august-17-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-094421171.html) — Yahoo Finance

A memorandum of understanding between the US and Iran **expired Monday**, reviving concern that the conflict could reignite. Brent pushed above **$90** after President Trump said he did not see the war ending soon, with added focus on potential tolls on the Strait of Hormuz. The **30-year Treasury yield closed at 5.31%, its highest since June 2007**, while the 10-year finished at **4.72%** — a move driven by debt and supply anxiety rather than a growth scare. Energy-driven inflation risk plus a rising long end is the least comfortable combination for equity multiples.

### [Nvidia will back up to $105 billion in financing for an OpenAI data center in Ohio](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/nvidia-financing-open-ai-data-center-ohio.html) — CNBC

Nvidia, OpenAI, and SB Energy announced the **PORTS-Pike Technology Campus**, an 8-gigawatt Ohio complex that SB Energy will build and own and OpenAI will lease for 20 years. Initial capacity is **4.25 GW** with an option for **3.75 GW** more; the first 800 MW is targeted for 2028. The site will run exclusively on Nvidia GPUs — as many as **1.5 million chips**, which Nvidia framed as roughly **$150–200 billion of potential revenue per generation** deployed through 2030. It is also another instance of the vendor-financing structures that drew analyst scrutiny last week: Nvidia is guaranteeing the financing for its own end demand.

### [New 50% Section 338 tariffs on select Canadian goods take effect Wednesday](https://www.mltaikins.com/insights/what-you-need-to-know-about-the-section-338-proclamations/) — MLT Aikins

Three proclamations signed July 20 invoke **Section 338 of the Tariff Act of 1930**, imposing an additional **50% tariff** on a range of Canadian imports effective **12:01 a.m. ET on August 19**. Coverage centers on motor vehicles, alcoholic beverages, and dairy, but extends to cement, plywood, furniture, and clothing. USTR puts exposure near **$20 billion**, about **5.2%** of the $382 billion the US imported from Canada in 2025. The duty applies regardless of CUSMA eligibility and is assessed on entry date, not ship date.

## Market Outlook

Equities gave back ground on a day when the pressure came from outside the equity market itself: an expired US-Iran understanding pushed Brent above **$90** and helped drive the 30-year Treasury yield to **5.31%**, its highest since June 2007, even as semiconductors led on Nvidia's **$105 billion** Ohio financing commitment. That divergence is the story — the AI capex engine is still firing, but it is now being financed into a long end that is repricing debt and supply, and rising energy costs feed directly back into the inflation path the Fed is watching. Ray Dalio's CAPE-41 warning and the vendor-financing structures behind the Ohio deal are two framings of the same question: whether reported AI gains are converting into cash at the pace valuations assume. With **Wednesday's FOMC minutes**, the **Section 338 Canadian tariffs taking effect the same morning**, and **Nvidia's earnings nine sessions out**, the near-term path runs through catalysts that speak to policy, input costs, and AI monetization at once. A rising long end alongside a 6.6% jump in the VIX from a low base warrants attention rather than alarm — but it leaves little cushion if any of those three disappoints.

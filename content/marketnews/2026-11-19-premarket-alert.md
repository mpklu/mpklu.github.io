## Overnight Developments

### [Anthropic prices a record $81.3 billion IPO and debuts on the Nasdaq today](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-ipo-ai-offering-price.html) — CNBC
Anthropic priced its IPO at **$83 a share** Wednesday, above the upwardly revised $70–$78 range. It also upsized the deal to about **980 million shares**, which takes the raise to **$81.3 billion**. That beats SpaceX's June IPO as the largest on record. The company's fully diluted market cap is about **$1.5 trillion**, and it starts trading today under the ticker **ANT**.

[Nasdaq-100 futures jumped](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) ahead of the debut. The rally comes after a rough stretch for AI: the S&P 500 fell for a **fifth straight session** Wednesday, its longest slide since June, and the Dow lost **1.1%**. Anthropic proxies are leading pre-market. Alphabet and Amazon each hold stakes worth more than $100 billion, and Broadcom is a major partner.
Affected sectors: AI, Big Tech, Semiconductors

### [Intel signs the first outside customer for 18A](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/intel-first-18a-foundry-customer.html) — CNBC
CEO Lip-Bu Tan said Wednesday evening that Intel has signed its first external foundry customer for 18A, without naming the client. Intel's technology chief said the deal is for the 18A-P node. Tan also named Microsoft as the first public customer for Intel's EMIB advanced packaging. Intel has lost more than half its value since the end of September.
Affected sectors: Semiconductors

### [Fed minutes: "almost all" officials expect more hikes](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/fed-minutes-october-2026.html) — CNBC
The October minutes came out Wednesday afternoon. They show "almost all participants" saw further rate increases as likely appropriate, and a couple raised the possibility of a larger move in December. Officials called AI demand "a material source of inflation risk". They also flagged that AI-related debt had grown rapidly through "complex and opaque financing structures."

Overnight, Reuters sources reported a missile hit on the PIF Tower in Riyadh. Oil is only modestly higher: Brent was at about **$92.43** (+0.5%) and WTI at **$83.74** (+0.6%).
Affected sectors: Rates-sensitive, AI infrastructure, Energy

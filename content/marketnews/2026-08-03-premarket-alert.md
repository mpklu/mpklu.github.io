## Overnight Developments

### [AstraZeneca and Bristol Myers Squibb explore a $400B merger](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/02/astrazeneca-and-bristol-myers-squibb-mull-400-billion-deal-report-.html) — CNBC / Financial Times

The *Financial Times* reported Sunday that AstraZeneca has held early-stage talks with Bristol Myers Squibb about a combination valued near **$400 billion** — which would create the world's fourth-largest pharmaceutical company and rank among the largest deals the industry has ever attempted. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-02/astrazeneca-is-said-to-have-explored-bristol-myers-mega-merger) confirmed the talks have run for several months, with sources cautioning the deal may still be delayed or fall apart. Neither company has confirmed the reports.

The market reaction inverted the usual acquirer/target script: **BMY rose** while **AZN fell as much as 7%**, with [analysts telling CNBC they were "perplexed"](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/astrazeneca-bristol-myers-squibb-merger-talks.html) by the strategic logic. Worth noting the history — AstraZeneca rebuffed Pfizer's $118B hostile bid in 2014, and is now reportedly chasing a cross-Atlantic deal several times that size. Any agreement would face simultaneous review by the FTC, the UK's CMA, and the European Commission.

Affected sectors: Healthcare, Pharmaceuticals

## Overnight Developments

### [US Strikes a Dozen Iranian Targets Overnight](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/30/oil-prices-us-iran-war.html) — CNBC
The US launched a fresh wave of strikes on roughly a dozen Iranian targets late Wednesday, retaliating for Iranian missile attacks on American forces in the region and ending the brief pause in hostilities that began in late July. Iran's Revolutionary Guard has threatened further escalation. Notably, oil is **not** confirming the escalation — Brent is *down* 0.7% to $90.10 and WTI down 1.2% to $83.49, suggesting traders have largely priced in a conflict that has run since late February. Gold at $4,140 (+2.6%) is the clearer risk signal.
Affected sectors: Energy, Shipping, Defense, Aviation

### [Fed Holds at 3.50–3.75%; 30-Year Yield Hits Highest Since 2007](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/29/treasury-yields-fed-interest-rates.html) — CNBC
The Fed held rates steady Wednesday in a divided **9–3 vote**, with three officials dissenting in favor of a *hike* and a firm warning on elevated inflation. The bond market revolted: the 30-year yield jumped ~10.5bp and touched **5.244%**, its highest level since July 2007, while the 10-year climbed to ~4.67%. Swaps now imply roughly a **60% chance of a September hike**. The Dow fell 800 points on the decision.
Affected sectors: Financials, Rate-sensitive equities, Housing, Utilities

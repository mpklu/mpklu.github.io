## Overnight Developments

### [Intel Slides as TSMC Explores Terafab Tie-Up](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/intel-stock-slides-as-tsmc-explores-terafab-tieup-analyst-flags-share-losses-4931558) — Investing.com

Published this morning at 6:40am ET. TSMC is reportedly exploring a partnership with Elon Musk's **Terafab** venture, the Texas semiconductor project where **Intel had been the sole named manufacturing partner** since April 2026. The proposed structure would have TSMC own and operate the facility with Terafab as anchor customer. Musk confirmed talks are ongoing but early — *"Just discussions, but something may come of it."*

The same report carries a second negative: Susquehanna estimates Intel kept losing CPU share in Q3, with laptop model share down **3.9% q/q to 68.1%** while AMD gained 3.1% to 29.3%. Desktop share fell to roughly **76%**, the lowest in the firm's study since Q2 2023.

This lands on a stock that has been extraordinarily volatile — INTC ran from $97 on Sep 14 to $127 on Sep 24, then chopped back to $119. Affected sectors: Semiconductors, Foundry.

### Nikkei Tags 70,000 for the First Time Since July

Japan closed **+2.40% at 69,946.86**, up 1,637 points, after briefly reclaiming the 70,000 handle for the first time in about three months. The driver is a catch-up to Friday's softer-than-expected US employment data, which pushed investors to scale back bets on another Fed hike at the October meeting, plus a rotation back into AI names — SoftBank +~4%, Tokyo Electron +5.6%, Advantest +4.8%.

Worth keeping in proportion: this is Asia repricing Friday's US data, not a fresh overnight catalyst for US markets. Affected sectors: Semiconductors, AI.

### Backdrop: European Sovereign Stress (dated Oct 1–2, not overnight)

French 10-year OAT yields briefly topped **5%** last week, the highest since July 2002, with the spread over Bunds widening to roughly **131–154bp** — a post-euro-crisis high, widest since 2011–2012. This is Friday's story rather than an overnight development, but it remains a live risk under European trading and is worth watching for spillover.

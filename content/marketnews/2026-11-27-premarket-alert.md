## Overnight Developments

### [Chinese chip stocks notch biggest-ever one-day gains as Beijing signals broader market support](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/china-chip-stocks-beijing-market-support.html) — CNBC
Premier Li Qiang chaired a State Council meeting on Friday morning. Officials pledged to give the **"National Team"** (China's state-backed funds) a bigger role in stabilizing markets and keeping liquidity ample. Per CNBC, it was the first time a State Council meeting had named the National Team explicitly. After the state media readout came out around midnight ET, the **CSI 300 rose 4%**, its biggest jump since October 2024. The **CSI Semiconductor index rose 17.6%**, its biggest one-day gain on record, and the STAR index rose 14%. The rally came one day after the CSI 300 closed at its lowest level since the tariff escalation in spring 2025.
Affected sectors: Semiconductors, Tech, China-exposed names, Materials

### [Two Italians killed in Kuwait; Israel says it is ready to strike Iran "at any moment"](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-italian-killed-trump.html) — CNBC
Kuwait's army blamed Iranian attacks for missile debris that killed two Italians. Tehran denied any role and called the incident "a precooked recipe for war." Israel's military spokesman said Israel could strike Iran without US forces. Late Thursday, Trump said the US had "victory at hand" and that the war would end "whenever I want it to be." Oil and defense are the sectors to watch for any escalation over the weekend.
Affected sectors: Energy, Defense, Airlines

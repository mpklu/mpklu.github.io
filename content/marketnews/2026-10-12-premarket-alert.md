## Overnight Developments

### [Houthi Strikes on Riyadh Airport Kill 12, Trump Weighs Joining Saudi Retaliation](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/11/trump-saudi-arabia-iran-houthis.html) — CNBC

Iran-backed Houthi militants struck King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh on Saturday, killing **12 people and injuring 309**, according to the Saudi General Authority of Civil Aviation. A second projectile hit the same passenger terminal on Sunday, killing one more and injuring nearly a dozen. It was the second deadly assault on the airport in under a week — three Saudi nationals, including a pilot, were killed there on Thursday. Saudi Arabia said Monday it was still dealing with incoming drones and missiles, and the aviation authority extended the suspension of airport operations.

The escalation matters for three reasons. First, President Trump said he is considering joining the Saudi response: *"We may. We're going to look at it,"* he told reporters, a move that would pull the US into what CNBC describes as a second front in the Iran war. Two Saudi officials told MS NOW the kingdom is requesting **"urgent defense support"** from Washington "as soon as possible." Second, the Houthis explicitly warned airlines and travelers away from all Saudi airports and airspace except Mecca and Medina, and Australia, New Zealand, the UK, the US, Canada, France, Germany and Japan have all issued travel advisories. Third, this is landing while Riyadh hosts the World Petroleum Congress, the International Energy Forum Ministerial and the Future Investment Initiative.

There is a diplomatic thread running the other way: Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi called his Saudi counterpart Sunday offering to help open a dialogue between Riyadh and the Houthis, and Tehran says it is still reviewing a US response to its proposal for reopening the Strait of Hormuz.

Affected sectors: Energy, Defense, Airlines, Travel

### [Trump Declares a Russia–Ukraine "Energy Ceasefire" — Oil Rose Anyway](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/12/oil-prics-today-iran-us-saudi-arabia-crude-.html) — CNBC

*"Effective immediately, there is an ENERGY CEASEFIRE in the War between Russia and Ukraine. Both parties have agreed. Do not break it!"* Trump said in a social media post Sunday. Zelenskyy welcomed it but warned that Moscow must honor it too. That follows Trump's statement Friday that Russia will supply more than **4 million tons of diesel** to the global market under a deal struck with Putin by phone.

Every piece of that should be bearish for crude. Prices did fall about **1.5%** early in the session — and then reversed. Brent for December delivery is back above **$105** and WTI for November is above **$92**. When a supply-positive headline of that size can't hold oil down, the market is telling you the risk premium is doing the work. UOB said Saturday's Riyadh strikes have *"materially elevated Middle East risk premia."*

Affected sectors: Energy, Transports, Industrials

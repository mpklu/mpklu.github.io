## Overnight Developments

### [U.S. strikes Iranian rocket launchers on the Strait of Hormuz](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/national-security/us-forces-strike-iranian-rocket-launchers-strait-hormuz-first-military-rcna595194) — NBC News

U.S. forces struck two Iranian rocket launchers on Larak Island in the Strait of Hormuz on Sunday, the first American strikes on Iran since late July and a break in a month-long lull. CENTCOM spokesperson Tim Hawkins said Revolutionary Guard forces had been observed preparing to fire rockets carrying sea mines into the strait — the same shipping lanes the U.S. military finished clearing of mines only last week. The IRGC confirmed the strikes hit Larak, acknowledged "the martyrdom and injury of several of our fighters and compatriots," and warned of a "retaliatory response."

Iran followed through overnight, [targeting U.S. military bases in Jordan and the United Arab Emirates](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/08/30/politics/us-iran-strikes-larak-island) with ballistic missiles and helicopters. The exchange marks the first direct escalation in roughly a month, and it lands days after the Trump administration said it would pivot from military action toward economic pressure to end the conflict.

Affected sectors: Energy, Shipping/Logistics, Defense, Airlines

## What to Watch at Open

Watch whether this stays an oil story or becomes an equity story — crude is up nearly 4% while S&P futures are down less than a third of a percent, and that gap is the whole question.

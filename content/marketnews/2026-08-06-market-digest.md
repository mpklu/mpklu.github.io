## Breaking News & Macro Events

### [Oil gains pressure stocks as Dow slips from record](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-thursday-august-6-dow-sp-nasdaq-091620000.html) — Yahoo Finance
Crude reversed a three-session slide with a sharp move higher: WTI rose 3.4% to $77.81 and Brent jumped 4.5% to $83.06. The backdrop remains the Strait of Hormuz, where a proposed Iran–Oman shipping-route arrangement — reportedly still in final drafting and explicitly *not* a full reopening of the waterway — has yet to be ratified. Optimism over that deal is what had been pushing crude lower into midweek; today's reversal suggests traders are re-pricing the durability of it. Rising energy costs plus a firmer 10-year yield (4.67%, +5bp) made for an unhelpful combination for equities.

### [Honeywell slashes 2026 outlook](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/05/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) — CNBC
Honeywell Aerospace shares fell roughly 20% after the company cut its 2026 organic sales growth forecast to 4–5% from 7–9%. As a Dow component with a high share price, this was the single largest contributor to the index's 464-point decline. Salesforce fell about 3% on a leadership shuffle, adding to the drag. Outside the Dow, AppLovin tumbled nearly 20% on mixed quarterly results and Western Digital shed 13% on weak guidance — evidence that the earnings-reaction environment is punishing any guidance blemish severely.

### [Google's AI leadership reshuffle](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/05/google-chief-scientist-jeff-dean-leaving-company-after-27-years.html) — CNBC
Announced late on August 5: Chief Scientist Jeff Dean is leaving Google after 27 years, departing with Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals, and Quoc Le to launch Discovery Loop, a public-benefit corporation focused on AI for science and engineering. Google will invest in the venture. Separately, Demis Hassabis [moved from CEO of Google DeepMind to Chair](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/google-deepmind-boss-hassabis-moves-to-chair-role-in-shakeup) while adding the Chief Scientist title at Alphabet, with Koray Kavukcuoglu elevated to SVP reporting to Sundar Pichai. Alphabet fell about 4% on the announcement and extended losses another 1% today — a two-day drawdown of roughly 5% that warrants attention given how much of the stock's multiple rests on AI execution.

## Market Outlook

Today's tape was narrow damage dressed up as a broad decline: the Dow's 464-point drop came overwhelmingly from Honeywell's 20% collapse on a slashed 2026 outlook and weakness in high-priced names like UnitedHealth, while the S&P 500 and NASDAQ finished within a fifth of a percent of flat and the VIX actually fell to 15.15. The more meaningful signal is the combination of crude reversing higher — WTI +3.4%, Brent +4.5% — alongside gold at $4,303 and the 10-year back up to 4.67%, which points to a geopolitical risk premium rebuilding around an unratified Strait of Hormuz arrangement rather than any improvement in growth expectations. Underneath, the earnings-reaction function has turned unforgiving: Honeywell, AppLovin, and Western Digital were all punished severely for guidance rather than results, and UPS remains under pressure a week after beating on revenue and EPS because margins compressed to 4.1%. That asymmetry warrants caution into a heavy watchlist calendar — Oklo reports tomorrow, Rocket Lab and Hims & Hers on Monday, Super Micro Tuesday — where a beat may not be enough if the outlook wobbles. The immediate catalyst is tomorrow morning's July jobs report, where consensus of 79K payrolls and an unchanged 4.2% unemployment rate sets a low bar, but any upside surprise in average hourly earnings would land on a 10-year yield that has already backed up five basis points.

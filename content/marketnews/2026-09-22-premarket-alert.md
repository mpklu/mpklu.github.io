## Overnight Developments

### [Iran offers to reopen Strait of Hormuz within seven days if US eases military pressure](https://news.sbs.co.kr/english/article.do?news_id=N1008766299) — Kyodo News (via SBS)
A senior Iranian government official told Kyodo that Tehran has proposed, through a mediator, to reopen the Strait of Hormuz within a week if Washington takes initial steps to reduce military pressure — specifically moving toward ending the blockade of Iranian ports and halting operations around the strait. The stated goal is to revive stalled end-of-war negotiations. The same official ruled out a Pezeshkian–Trump meeting on the sidelines of this week's UN General Assembly, so a quick handshake is off the table. No US response yet.

Crude broke sharply lower on the report at roughly 04:00 ET, one discontinuous step on a volume spike across every contract month. Against Monday's settlement, **November WTI is -3.2% to ~$89.40** (from $92.37) and **November Brent is -2.7% to ~$97.60** (from $100.34). This extends the Hormuz-driven slide that took WTI below $100 last week — Monday's alert covered improving tanker traffic; today's move is a formal diplomatic offer, which is a different and larger signal. Gold barely moved (-0.4%), so this is an oil story, not a broad risk-off/on rotation.

Affected sectors: Energy (producers, refiners, oilfield services), Airlines & Transports (fuel-cost relief), Shipping/Tankers, Defense.

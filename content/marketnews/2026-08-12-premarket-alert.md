## Overnight Developments

No macro or geopolitical events overnight. This morning's move is entirely earnings-driven, concentrated in AI infrastructure.

### [Supermicro Q4 FY2026 Results](https://ir.supermicro.com/news/news-details/2026/Supermicro-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Fiscal-Year-2026-Financial-Results/default.aspx) — Super Micro Computer

SMCI reported after the close yesterday. Q4 net sales came in at **$11.1 billion** (vs. $10.2B in Q3'26 and $5.8B a year ago), with gross margin at **17.5%** — up from 9.9% last quarter and 9.5% a year ago. Net income was **$1,178 million** against $195 million in Q4'25. Full-year FY2026 revenue landed at $39.1 billion, nearly double FY2025's $22.0 billion.

The bigger driver is guidance: the company guided FY2027 revenue to **$65–72 billion against analyst consensus near $54.4 billion**, on the back of more than $60 billion in new orders and record backlog. Q1 FY2027 is guided to $14.5–15.5 billion.

Affected sectors: Semiconductors, AI infrastructure, IT hardware

### [CoreWeave Q2 Results](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/11/coreweave-crwv-q2-earnings-report-2026.html) — CNBC

CoreWeave (not on the watchlist, but the read-through matters) posted Q2 revenue of **$2.58 billion vs. $2.56 billion expected**, up 112% year over year, with an adjusted loss of $1.03 per share against a $1.20 expected loss. Backlog stands at $104 billion, with new business from Anthropic and Meta announced in the quarter. Q3 guidance of $3.45–3.6 billion came in at or above the high end of estimates. Shares rose roughly 14% after hours.

Affected sectors: AI infrastructure, cloud, data center

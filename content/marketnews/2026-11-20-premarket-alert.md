## Overnight Developments

### [SoftBank suffers worst day in over two years as AI sell-off hits Asian tech stocks](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/softbank-ai-tech-stocks-slide-anthropic-debut.html) — CNBC
Thursday's AI selloff spread through Asia overnight. SoftBank Group closed **14.48% lower**, its worst day since August 2024. The stock is now down 31% this month on worries about its $64.6 billion exposure to OpenAI. Japanese chip stocks also fell hard: Tokyo Electron lost 7.06%, Lasertec 9.21% and Renesas 7.02%. In Korea, SK Hynix fell 5.92% and Samsung Electronics 7.01%. **TSMC closed 4.76% lower in Taiwan**, and the Nikkei 225 fell **5.06%** while the Kospi fell **5.21%**.
Affected sectors: Semiconductors, AI infrastructure, Tech

### [Nasdaq futures drop as Anthropic falls one day after debut, chip stocks decline](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/19/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) — CNBC
Anthropic was **down 6.37% in premarket trading** at 5 a.m. ET, a day after it [closed its record IPO debut at $97.17](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/anthropic-stock-ai-amd-nvidia.html). AMD slid again after [short seller Hunterbrook alleged](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/19/amd-falls-on-short-seller-report-of-alleged-china-exposure.html) that AMD firmware kept reaching a blacklisted Chinese server maker. AMD calls the claims a misrepresentation and says it complies with U.S. export law. The stock closed down 7.72% on Thursday. The Dow is down **6.2% week to date**, which puts it on pace for its worst week since April 2025. The S&P 500 and Nasdaq are each down about 5% for the week. On the other side, Deutsche Bank's Binky Chadha called the market "very oversold." Treasuries rallied as investors sought safety: the 10-year yield fell 6 basis points to 4.989%, back below 5%.
Affected sectors: Tech, Semiconductors

### [Japan core inflation rises to 2.2%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/japan-inflation-cpi-october.html) — CNBC
Japan's October core CPI came in at 2.2%, a little below the 2.3% forecast. Energy prices rose 2.2% after household subsidies expired. LSEG data puts the chance of a **Bank of Japan hike in December at 72%**. The yen is near 159.6 per dollar.
Affected sectors: Financials, FX

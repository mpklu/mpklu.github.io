## Breaking News & Macro Events

### [Jensen Huang reinstated as Nvidia CEO. Stock skyrockets 31%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/nvidia-jensen-huang-ceo.html) — CNBC
Nvidia's board **voted unanimously** to reinstate Huang, effective immediately, 11 days after he resigned. The board said it had "received input from senior levels of the United States government" and is confident his return "poses no national security risk." CNBC's sources say government officials briefed the board late last week that no national security laws were broken. Huang and the board first spoke on Saturday and agreed on Sunday that he would return. The stock rose **31.5%** to $165.05 on **772 million shares**. Its Nov. 6 close was $186.64, so it is still about 12% below where it stood before the scandal. Bank of America [reiterated its buy rating](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/big-monday-wall-street-calls-like-anthropic.html) but warned that "volatility [will] stay elevated until there is greater clarity on the SEC inquiry, the 10-Q, and CY27 cloud capex plans."

### [Trump tells CNBC 'I made the deal' to reinstate Huang; Congress pushes back](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/trump-nvidia-jensen-huang.html) — CNBC
"I spoke to them, let me put it that way," Trump told CNBC's Eamon Javers. "And I convinced them. I made the deal." He said the board "did ask for assurance on the national security threat," and that the administration gave it. Lawmakers from both parties [condemned the decision](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/nvidia-jensen-huang-congress-trump.html). Rep. Don Bacon called it "a failure of character" and said Commerce Secretary Lutnick may be responsible. Huang's closed-door House testimony starts **Dec. 1**, and the Senate Banking Committee announced a **public hearing on Dec. 3**. Late in the session, CNBC [reported](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/11/23/megaspeed-to-be-added-to-commerce-departments-trade-blacklist-source.html) that Commerce plans to add Megaspeed, the Singapore firm tied to Alice Huang, to its trade blacklist. The leadership question is settled for now, but the export-control and governance questions remain open.

### [The AI trade comes back: memory, neoclouds and chips lead](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/stocks-moving-the-most-today-live-updates.html) — CNBC
CoreWeave and Dell each gained about **22%**, Micron and SK Hynix about 20%, Bloom Energy 19% and Lumentum 18%. Nebius rose 29% and IREN 27%. Those neoclouds had lost 37% and nearly 40% over the past month on fears about Nvidia's **$36 billion** in off-balance-sheet backstops. The VanEck Semiconductor ETF (SMH) was [up more than 16%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/22/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) during the session, on pace for its best day since April 2025. Gold miners also rallied, with Newmont, AngloGold, Coeur and Gold Fields each up more than 7%. Boeing rose 4% after Trump said China would buy another 250 jets. The notable laggard was **Anthropic**, which fell 1.4% to $87.64, its second straight loss after Thursday's 17% debut gain. "As a leading AI stock, if you can't rally today, something's not right," said Eric Diton of The Wealth Alliance.

### [Fed's Williams: higher rates likely 'over time'; December hike now ~98% priced](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/john-williams-fed-inflation-rate-hikes.html) — CNBC (Jeff Cox)
NY Fed President John Williams said "getting inflation back to 2% is 100% essential" and "there is still work to do." He blamed current pressure on the Iran conflict, AI-driven demand for goods, and tariffs, and said the tariff effects have "largely abated." He did not address December directly. This is expected to be the last major Fed speech before the Dec. 8–9 meeting. Per CME FedWatch, traders now price a [~98% chance of a 25 bp hike](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/treasury-yields-bonds-fed.html), up from ~88% Friday. The 2-year yield rose more than 7 bp to 4.69%. Macquarie's Thierry Wizman said a 50 bp hike "now feels like an impossibility." In data, S&P Global's flash PMIs [slowed](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/22/stock-market-today-live-updates.html): manufacturing fell to 53.7 from 54.5 and services to 54.7 from 56.1. Both are still consistent with about **3% Q4 GDP growth**.

### [Morgan Stanley's Wilson: 'This is the fat pitch'](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/morgan-stanley-says-this-is-a-big-buying-opportunity-were-not-going-to-miss-it.html) — CNBC (Deena Zaidi)
Mike Wilson wrote, "This is the fat pitch we have patiently been waiting for, and we're not going to miss it." Morgan Stanley's sentiment indicator fell to **-1.6**, matching its December 2018 lows. He said Friday's 6,910 close was an "overshoot of fair value" below the bank's 7,100–7,300 range. The S&P was at **16.4x** forward earnings, against his fair-value estimate of 19–19.6x. Prices implied a 6% drop in 2027 S&P earnings, while the bank forecasts **14% growth**. He favors AI hyperscalers, industrials and quality cyclicals. He sees a hawkish Fed as the main risk, but argued that falling oil and moderating growth may make market rate expectations "too hawkish."

### [CrowdStrike to buy Zscaler for $43.9 billion](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/22/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) — CNBC
The all-stock deal creates one of the largest pure-play cybersecurity companies. CrowdStrike holders would own about **84.1%** of the combined company. Reuters reported about $10 billion in combined ARR and $1 billion in annualized revenue synergies. In pre-market trading Zscaler rose about 14% and CrowdStrike fell about 8%.

## Market Outlook

Huang's reinstatement produced the best day for the S&P 500 (+4.7%) and the Nasdaq (+6.5%) since April 2025. Nvidia rose 31%, the VIX fell 26% to 26.6, and the S&P recovered a little under half of last week's 8.6% drop in one session. The quality of the rally deserves caution. The indexes made most of their gain at the open. The Nasdaq closed below its opening level. Speculative names on the watchlist, including RKLB, OKLO, SOFI, HIMS and CRCL, closed at or near their session lows. Anthropic fell in an AI rally. The policy backdrop is also less supportive than the tape. The 2-year yield rose more than 7 bp as December hike odds climbed to about 98% after Williams's comments, and Nvidia's Dec. 1 and Dec. 3 hearings plus the SEC inquiry keep its governance questions open. Wednesday's October PCE (consensus +0.3% m/m) and revised Q3 GDP will be the last major data before the Dec. 9 decision. A firm PCE print would test whether today was a turn or the relief rally many strategists expected.

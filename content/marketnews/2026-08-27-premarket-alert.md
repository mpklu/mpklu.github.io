## Overnight Developments

### [Nvidia's blowout Q2 reignites the AI trade](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/26/nvidia-nvda-earnings-report-q2-2027-live-updates.html) — CNBC

Nvidia reported after Wednesday's close and beat on every line that mattered: **$2.22 EPS** on **$96.2B revenue, up 106% year over year**. The guidance did the real damage to the bear case — CFO Colette Kress pointed to fiscal 2028 revenue growth well above consensus, and management disclosed that **AWS will buy 2 million Nvidia GPUs** along with the new Vera CPU. That single datapoint undercuts the "hyperscalers are tapping out" thesis that has hung over the AI complex since spring. Nvidia also returned **$26B via buybacks and dividends** in the quarter.

Affected sectors: Semiconductors, AI infrastructure, Cloud, Software

### The tape agrees, but only in one place

This is a **narrow, tech-only** move. NASDAQ futures are up nearly 1% while Dow futures are *negative* — the rotation is inside the market, not a broad risk-on lift. Asia mostly shrugged: the Nikkei and Hang Seng both closed lower despite having the Nvidia print in hand.

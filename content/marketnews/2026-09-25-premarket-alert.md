## Overnight Developments

### [Akamai signs $11.6 billion, seven-year cloud infrastructure deal with Anthropic](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/akamai-announces-11-6-billion-200100248.html) — Yahoo Finance
Announced at **4:01 PM ET Thursday**, minutes after the close: Akamai will supply Anthropic with cloud infrastructure under **$11.6 billion of contractual commitment over seven years**, expandable by up to another $9 billion for a total potential commitment of roughly **$20 billion**. Akamai also issued Anthropic a warrant for non-voting convertible preferred representing **7.7 million shares — about 5% of shares outstanding — at an exercise price of $111.33**, struck essentially at Thursday's $110.41 close. Akamai is indicated **+19.3% at $131.66** in pre-market trade.

This is the catalyst behind the Nasdaq's outperformance. It re-rates the "AI capacity landlord" trade beyond the hyperscalers — a CDN operator just booked an AI commitment worth more than three times its trailing annual revenue, and the warrant structure hands the customer equity upside rather than cash.
Affected sectors: Tech, Cloud Infrastructure, Semiconductors, AI

## Overnight Developments

### [Nvidia slides again as Bessent calls for a "dramatic shakeup"](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/15/bessent-nvidia-ceo-lutnick-megaspeed.html) — CNBC
Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said on Sunday that Nvidia's board should look outside the company for its next CEO after Jensen Huang resigned last week. He also asked whether the company had pushed "seemingly illicit sales" to boost revenue, which points to export-control scrutiny of other executives. CNBC reported Nvidia [down 3.65% pre-market as of 7:04 a.m. ET](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/nvidia-stock-price-jensen-huang.html). The stock fell 13% last week, its worst week since April 2025. Interim CEO Colette Kress reports Q3 results after the bell on **Tuesday**.
Affected sectors: Semiconductors, AI hardware

### [Asia's AI supply chain sells off](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/15/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) — CNBC
The Nvidia fallout spread across Asia's chip suppliers. Taiwan's TAIEX closed **-6.0%**, TSMC's Taipei shares fell **5.7%**, South Korea's Kospi lost **3.6%** and Japan's Nikkei 225 fell **2.4%**. CNBC reported SK Hynix down more than 5.6%, Advantest down almost 10% and Tokyo Electron down 5.2%.
Affected sectors: Semiconductors, semi equipment

### Weak macro prints from Japan and China — CNBC
[Japan's Q3 GDP unexpectedly shrank](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/japan-q3-gdp-energy-oil-prices.html) at a 0.1% annualized rate against a forecast of +0.3% growth. Expensive energy imports were the main drag. [China's October data missed across the board](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/china-retail-sales-october-industrial-output-investment.html): retail sales rose 0.4% (forecast 0.8%), industrial output grew 4.8% (forecast 5.3%), and fixed-asset investment is down 8.1% year to date.
Affected sectors: Global cyclicals, materials

### [Iran fires drones at U.S. bases in Bahrain](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/15/bahrain-iran-drone-attacks-tehran-us-base.html) — CNBC
Iran fired drones on Sunday at U.S. bases in Bahrain, which hosts the Fifth Fleet's headquarters. In Yemen, Saudi-backed forces pulled back from the Bab el-Mandeb Strait. Oil has not reacted much: WTI futures are down about 0.9% this morning.
Affected sectors: Energy, defense

### [Bitcoin tests its 2026 lows](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/bitcoin-technical-warning-level.html) — CNBC
Bitcoin touched $62,000 over the weekend and was near $64,200 this morning. 22V Research strategist John Roque is watching the $60,000 neckline and says a break below it "would then imply risk to $38,000."
Affected sectors: Crypto, bitcoin-treasury stocks
